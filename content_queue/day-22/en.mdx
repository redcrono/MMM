## 1. How South Korea Calculates Automobile Tax & Why January Prepayment Wins

In South Korea, every registered vehicle owner is assessed local **Automobile Tax (자동차세)** plus a 30% **Local Education Tax (지방교육세)** surcharge twice a year—First Half in June and Second Half in December.
- **Internal Combustion & Hybrid Vehicles:** Taxed per cubic centimeter (cc) of engine displacement (`₩104/cc` up to 1,000cc; `₩182/cc` up to 1,600cc; and `₩260/cc` over 1,600cc, including the 30% education surcharge). Beginning from the **3rd year after initial vehicle registration**, your car tax automatically drops by **5% per year** up to a maximum **50% seniority discount** in Year 12+.
- **100% Battery Electric Vehicles (EVs) & Hydrogen Vehicles:** Assessed a flat annual tax of **₩130,000** (₩100,000 base + ₩30,000 education tax) regardless of motor horsepower or vehicle price.

By utilizing the **Annual Prepayment System (자동차세 연납 신청)** in January, you pay the entire year's tax in a single lump sum and receive a **5.0% statutory discount on the remaining 11 months (Feb–Dec)**—which equals **4.57% off your total annual bill**. In an era where 1-year bank deposits pay ~3.3% *before* 15.4% interest tax, locking in a guaranteed **4.57% tax-free return** in January is a no-brainer for every car owner.

## 3. Double Dipping: Stacking Credit Card Cashback & Local Mileage

Unlike national income taxes (which charge a 0.8% credit card processing fee), **Local Taxes (지방세)** such as Automobile Tax have **0% credit card surcharge**! Even better, you can stack three extra benefits on top of the 4.57% prepayment discount:
1. **Interest-Free Installments & Cashback Events:** Major Korean card issuers (Shinhan, KB Kookmin, Hyundai, Samsung, Lotte) offer **2-to-3-month interest-free installments** on local tax payments exceeding ₩50,000, or provide coffee coupons and ₩5,000–₩10,000 cashback when you register for their January local tax event before paying.
2. **Seoul E-Tax Mileage (서울시 ETAX 마일리지):** If your vehicle is registered in Seoul and you opt into electronic notifications + automatic transfer/card payment on `etax.seoul.go.kr`, you earn an additional **₩800 to ₩1,600 in E-Tax mileage** that converts directly into T-Money or cash reductions.
3. **One-Time Registration Convenience:** Once you pay your annual car tax in January, your municipality **automatically mails you a discounted January prepayment bill every subsequent year** without requiring you to re-apply—unless you purchase a new vehicle with a new license plate.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Why can't I pay my car tax via Automatic Bank Transfer (자동이체) in January?
This is the #1 reason people accidentally lose their January discount! Even if you have registered an automatic bank transfer for regular local taxes, **Automobile Tax Annual Prepayment is classified as a voluntary application bill, NOT a regular tax assessment**, meaning automatic withdrawal often does **not** trigger in most municipalities (except where specifically configured in Seoul E-Tax). Always log into WeTax, KakaoPay, or your banking app between January 16 and January 31 to manually click "Pay"!

### Q2. What happens if I move from Gyeonggi-do to Seoul after prepaying my car tax in January?
**Nothing—you do not pay twice.** Automobile tax prepayment records are synchronized nationwide across all municipal tax databases via the Ministry of the Interior and Safety's WeTax network. Moving to another city or province mid-year requires zero additional tax payment for that calendar year.

### Q3. Does paying Automobile Tax by credit card count toward my Year-End Tax Settlement (연말정산) credit card deduction?
**No.** Under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act, national taxes, local taxes (including Automobile Tax), utility bills, and apartment management fees are excluded from the Year-End Tax Settlement credit card deduction. However, many credit cards still count local tax payments toward your **monthly minimum spending requirement (전월실적)** or offer point accumulation—always check your card's specific terms.

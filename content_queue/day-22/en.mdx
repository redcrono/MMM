Under Article 128 of the Local Tax Act of South Korea, vehicle owners who prepay their full-year automobile tax in **January** instead of regular biannual installments (June and December) receive a statutory **4.57% net discount**.

      <li><strong>Digital Portals:</strong> Seoul registered vehicles use Seoul E-Tax (etax.seoul.go.kr); all other provinces use WeTax (wetax.go.kr).</li>
      <li><strong>Pro-Rata Refunds:</strong> Selling or scrapping your car during the year automatically triggers a pro-rata refund for unused days.</li>

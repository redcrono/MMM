The Ministry of SMEs and Startups has officially expanded the annual tax deduction cap of the **Yellow Umbrella Mutual Aid Fund (노란우산공제)** from ₩5.0 million to **₩6.0 million**. 

Operated by KBIZ, this government-backed mutual aid fund serves as a statutory severance retirement vehicle for self-employed entrepreneurs and freelancers in South Korea.

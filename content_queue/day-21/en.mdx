To assist sole proprietors burdened by crippling high-interest debt from second-tier lenders (savings banks, credit card loans, capital firms), the Korean government operates the **2026 Small Business Refinancing Policy Loan (소상공인 대환대출)**, converting debt yielding 7%+ into **fixed rates of 3.5% to 4.5%**.

      <li><strong>Target Debt:</strong> Business loans from secondary lenders exceeding 7% per annum held for over 3 months.</li>
      <li><strong>Cap & Rates:</strong> Up to ₩50 million per applicant at fixed policy rates of 3.5%-4.5%.</li>
      <li><strong>Maturity:</strong> Up to 10-year repayment terms (including up to 3 years grace period).</li>

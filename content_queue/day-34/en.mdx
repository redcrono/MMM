## 1. How South Korea's 2026 Fuel Tax Cut Extension Works at the Pump

Because South Korea imports nearly 100% of its crude oil, retail pump prices include a heavy layer of statutory fuel taxes (`유류세`, comprising Transportation-Energy-Environment Tax, Education Tax, Local Motor Fuel Tax, and 10% VAT). To shield commuters and small business drivers from global oil volatility, the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MOEF) operates a **Flexible Fuel Tax Cut (`유류세 탄력세율 인하`)**:

> **`2026 Pump Tax Reduction Breakdown (Including 10% VAT):`**
> - **`Gasoline (휘발유, -15% Cut): Tax reduced from KRW 820/L → KRW 698/L (Saves KRW 122 per liter)`**
> - **`Diesel (경유, -25% Cut): Tax reduced from KRW 581/L → KRW 436/L (Saves KRW 145 per liter)`**
> - **`LPG Butane (LPG 부탄, -25% Cut): Tax reduced from KRW 203/L → KRW 152/L (Saves KRW 51 per liter)`**

This baseline reduction is automatically baked into the retail pump price at every gas station across South Korea. To find stations that pass 100% of the tax cut on to consumers immediately, download the Korea National Oil Corporation's official **`Opinet (오피넷)`** mobile app and filter for **Alttul Gas Stations (`알뜰주유소`)**, which consistently price gasoline KRW 40–80/L below private brand stations.

## 2. Double Your Savings: The NTS KRW 300,000 Compact Car Fuel Tax Refund (`경차 유류세 환급`)

If you drive a Korean **Compact Car (`경차`, engine displacement under 1,000cc)**—such as the **Hyundai Casper (gasoline/turbo)**, **Kia Ray**, **Kia Morning (Picanto)**, **Chevrolet Spark**, or **Damas/Labo**—you are entitled to a separate, stackable **KRW 300,000 annual fuel tax refund** administered by the National Tax Service (NTS).

Unlike welfare subsidies, the Compact Car Fuel Refund has **zero income limit and zero net-worth limit**. Even if you earn KRW 150,000,000 per year, you qualify as long as your household meets the **1-Household 1-Compact-Car Rule**.

## 3. The 40% Penalty Trap: Strict Rules When Using Your Compact Car Fuel Card

Because the NTS subsidizes KRW 250 per liter out of national tax revenues, the government's POS monitoring system flags any transaction that exceeds the physical fuel tank capacity of a 1,000cc compact car (typically 35 to 38 liters):
1. **Per-Swipe Cap (KRW 60,000) & Daily Cap (KRW 120,000):** You can only receive the tax refund on up to **KRW 60,000 per single fueling session** and **KRW 120,000 per day**. Moreover, repeatedly swiping over **KRW 48,000 in a single fill-up** can trigger an automated audit verifying whether you are secretly fueling a larger sedan. Best practice: always stop the pump at **KRW 40,000 to KRW 45,000**.
2. **Never Refuel Another Car:** Using your Compact Car Fuel Card (`경차사랑카드`) to put gas into a friend's or spouse's vehicle results in **100% clawback of all refunded taxes plus a 40% punitive surtax (`부정수급 가산세 40%`)** and permanent disqualification.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Does an electric compact car (like the Hyundai Casper Electric or Kia Ray EV) qualify for the KRW 300,000 Compact Car Fuel Refund?
**A.** **No.** Because the program is legally a **refund of excise taxes levied on petroleum fuels (`교통·에너지·환경세 및 개별소비세 환급`)**, 100% battery-electric vehicles (EVs) do not pay petroleum fuel tax and therefore are excluded from the Compact Car Fuel Tax Refund Card. (Instead, EV owners qualify for Korea's Ministry of Environment EV Charging Card discounts and 50% expressway toll/public parking discounts.)

### Q2. Can a husband and wife who co-own one compact car (50/50 joint title, `공동명의`) apply for the card?
**A.** **Yes!** As long as the jointly owned compact car is the only vehicle in the household, either one of the co-owners can apply for the Compact Car Fuel Card and claim the full KRW 300,000 annual refund.

### Q3. What happens if I sell my compact car and buy another compact car mid-year?
**A.** When you replace one compact car with another, you must call your card issuer (Shinhan, Lotte, or Hyundai) and **update your license plate number (`차량번호 변경 신청`)** or have the card reissued for the new license plate. Any fuel pumped into the new car before updating the license plate with the card company will not receive the KRW 250/L refund.

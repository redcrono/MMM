## 1. The Unused Contribution Carryover Formula: Why December 31 Matters

The **Individual Savings Account (개인종합자산관리계좌 - ISA)** is South Korea's flagship all-in-one tax shelter, available to any **Tax Resident (거주자) aged 19 or older** (or working youth aged 15–18) who has not been subject to Comprehensive Financial Income Taxation (>₩20M annual interest/dividends) in the prior 3 years.

What makes the ISA unique in December is its **Cumulative Annual Allowance Carryover Rule (납입한도 이월 규정)**. Each calendar year you hold an active ISA adds **₩20,000,000** of contribution room (up to a lifetime principal cap of **₩100,000,000** over 5 years):

$$\text{Available Contribution Room in Year } N = (N \times \text{₩20,000,000}) - \text{Cumulative Past Deposits}$$

- **Scenario A (Open on December 30, 2026 with ₩0 deposited):** You immediately earn ₩20,000,000 of 2026 contribution room. Two days later, on **January 1, 2027**, your 2027 allowance (+₩20,000,000) stacks on top—giving you **₩40,000,000** of immediate tax-sheltered deposit capacity and starting your 3-year maturity clock early!
- **Scenario B (Wait until January 2, 2027 to open):** You lose the entire 2026 ₩20M tranche and are capped at just ₩20,000,000 for all of 2027.

## 3. What to Hold (and Avoid) Inside Your Korean Brokerage ISA

To squeeze every Won of tax alpha out of your ₩20M/year ISA contribution limit, prioritize assets that face heavy 15.4% dividend/gain taxation in a regular account:
1. **Top Priority #1 — KRX-Listed US Index & Dividend ETFs:**
   In a regular Korean brokerage account, capital gains and distributions from Korean-listed overseas ETFs (`TIGER US S&P500`, `KODEX US Nasdaq100`, `ACE US Dividend Dow Jones`) are taxed as **15.4% dividend income**. Inside a Brokerage ISA, 100% of those gains compound **tax-deferred** until maturity, and are then sheltered by your ₩2M/₩4M tax-free allowance and 9.9% separate tax!
2. **Top Priority #2 — High-Dividend Korean Stocks, REITs, Bonds & RP/ELBs:**
   Preferred stocks (`Samsung Electronics Pref 005935`), banking groups (`KB`, `Shinhan`, `Hana`, `Woori`), infrastructure funds (`Macquarie Korea Infrastructure`), and KRX REITs normally lose 15.4% of every quarterly payout to withholding taxes—inside an ISA, 100% of the gross dividend hits your cash balance untouched.
3. **What to Avoid — Non-Dividend Domestic Growth Stocks:**
   Because capital gains on domestic KOSPI/KOSDAQ stocks are already 0% tax-free in a regular account, do not waste your precious ₩20M ISA contribution limit on zero-dividend Korean biotech or small-cap growth stocks.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign expats living in South Korea open a Brokerage-Type ISA?
**Yes!** Under Article 91-18 of the Restriction of Special Taxation Act, any foreign national who qualifies as a **Tax Resident of South Korea (거주자 - domicile or 183+ days residence in Korea)** and is not subject to Comprehensive Financial Income Taxation is legally eligible to open an ISA. While some brokerages restrict mobile-only opening for Alien Registration Cards (ARCs), visiting a branch of Samsung Securities, Mirae Asset, or NH Investment with your ARC, passport, and NTS Income Certificate for ISA (`개인종합자산관리계좌 가입용 소득확인증명서` from HomeTax) allows full enrollment.

### Q2. If I deposit ₩20M this year and withdraw ₩5M for an emergency, can I re-deposit that ₩5M later this year?
**No.** The ₩20,000,000 annual limit and ₩100,000,000 total limit track **gross cumulative deposits**, not net balance. Withdrawing principal mid-term is penalty-free, but it does **not** restore the contribution room you already used.

### Q3. When should I NOT close and re-open my ISA at the 3-year mark?
If your outside taxable interest and dividend income exceeded **₩20,000,000** in any of the previous 3 years (making you a Comprehensive Financial Income Taxpayer), **do NOT close your ISA at the 3-year mark!** While existing ISA holders who become Comprehensive Taxpayers are allowed to keep and extend their current ISA maturity (e.g., extending maturity to age 80), closing the account will permanently block you from opening a new ISA until you have 3 consecutive years below ₩20M.

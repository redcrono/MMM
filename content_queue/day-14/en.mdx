Under South Korea's Ministry of Health and Welfare family support scheme, all households welcoming newborns receive **₩1,000,000 per month for infants under age 1 (0-11 months)** and **₩500,000 per month for age 1 (12-23 months)**, regardless of parental income.

      <li><strong>Daycare Enrollment:</strong> Daycare voucher deductions leave parents with the remaining difference (approx. ₩460,000/month for age 0) in cash.</li>
      <li><strong>Application Deadline:</strong> Apply within 60 days of birth for full retroactive payouts.</li>

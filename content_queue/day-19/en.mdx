Under South Korea's National Housing and Urban Fund, households giving birth within 2 years qualify for the **Newborn Special Mortgage Loan (신생아 특례 디딤돌 대출)**, offering an extraordinary **1.6% to 3.3% interest rate** and comprehensive refinancing rights.

Maximizing after-tax compound returns in South Korea requires strategically deploying the triumvirate of statutory tax shelters: **Brokerage ISA (중개형 ISA)**, **Pension Savings (연금저축펀드)**, and **Individual Retirement Pension (IRP)**.

      <li><strong>Pension Savings:</strong> Up to ₩6M annual tax credit (16.5% refund), 100% equity ETF flexibility.</li>
      <li><strong>IRP:</strong> Expands total credit cap to ₩9M; mandates 30% safe asset portfolio allocation.</li>
      <li><strong>Roll-Over Synergy:</strong> Transferring ISA maturity balance into Pension/IRP unlocks an extra 10% (up to ₩3M) tax credit bonus.</li>

South Korea levies a flat **22% capital gains tax (including local income tax)** on realized profits from overseas equities exceeding the annual **₩2,500,000 basic deduction**. 

Executing year-end **Tax-Loss Harvesting** prior to the December settlement cutoff allows investors to reset their cost basis and reduce their tax liability to zero.

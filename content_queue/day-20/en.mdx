Under Korean tax law, annual interest and dividend income up to **₩20,000,000** is withheld at a flat 15.4%. Exceeding ₩20,000,000 by even ₩1 triggers **Comprehensive Financial Income Taxation (up to 49.5% marginal rate)** and instant forfeiture of National Health Insurance dependent status.

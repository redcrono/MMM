## 1. The Free License Shield: National Police Agency's Good Driving Mileage (`착한운전 마일리지`)

Administered by the Korean National Police Agency (NPA) via **Traffic Civil Petition 24 (`교통민원24 / 이파인`, efine.go.kr)**, the **Good Driving Mileage (`착한운전 마일리지`)** program is a free statutory pledge open to anyone holding a valid South Korean driver's license (`운전면허증`).

Once you click "Pledge" inside the eFine app and complete 12 consecutive months with **zero traffic violations** (no fines, camera tickets, or suspensions) and **zero injury accidents**, the police credit your license with **10 Mileage Points** and automatically roll your pledge into the next year.

> **`License Suspension Defense Formula = If your cumulative traffic demerit points reach 40+ (the statutory threshold for a 40-day license suspension), redeem 10 Good Driving Mileage points to erase 10 demerit points and cancel the suspension!`**

**The "Paper Driver" Secret (`장롱면허 적립 꿀팁`):** You do **not** need to own a car or drive regularly to earn Good Driving Mileage points! As long as you hold a Korean driver's license, registering the pledge today means you automatically bank +10 points every year while taking the subway—building a 50- to 100-point safety buffer for when you eventually buy or rent a car.

## 3. Crucial for Expats: How to Cut Your First Korean Auto Policy by 30% Using Overseas Insurance History

When a foreign resident buys their first car in South Korea, Korean insurers default your domestic insurance history to **Year 1 (`가입경력 1년 미만`)**, quoting a steep beginner premium of KRW 1,500,000 to KRW 2,200,000 per year—even if you drove accident-free in the US, UK, Canada, or Australia for 10 years!

Under the **Overseas Auto Insurance History Recognition System (`해외 자동차보험 가입경력 인정제도`)**, you can slash up to **30%–40% off your Korean premium** by submitting three documents to your Korean insurer:
1. Official **Certificate of Overseas Auto Insurance Enrollment & Policy Period (`해외 보험가입증명서 / 무사고증명서`)** issued by your former insurer in English (showing exact start and end dates).
2. Copy of your passport and **Certificate of Entry & Exit (`출입국에 관한 사실증명`)** proving you resided in that country during the insured period.
3. Even if you already paid full price for your Korean car insurance over the past 1–3 years, you can submit these documents retroactively to receive a **cash refund of your overpaid premiums (`과납보험료 환급`)**!

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Does accumulating Police Good Driving Mileage (`착한운전 마일리지`) directly lower my private car insurance premium?
**A.** The two systems are separate: **Police Good Driving Mileage** protects your **driver's license from suspension** by offsetting demerit points (`벌점 공제`), whereas your **auto insurer's Mileage Rider** refunds cash based on **how few kilometers you drove (`주행거리 환급`)**. However, because earning Police Good Driving Mileage means you had zero traffic tickets and zero accidents for the year, your private insurer will also automatically award you the **Traffic Law Compliance Discount (`법규준수 할인`)** and upgrade your **Discount/Surcharge Grade (`할인할증등급`)** at renewal!

### Q2. What happens if I sign up for the Odometer Mileage Rider (`주행거리 특약`) and end up driving 25,000 km this year?
**A.** **You pay zero penalty!** The Odometer Mileage Rider is a pure upside post-settlement option (`사후환급형`). If you drive less than 15,000 km, you get a cash refund; if you drive more than 15,000 km, you simply receive KRW 0 refund with **no surcharge or disadvantage whatsoever**.

### Q3. If I get a speeding camera ticket (`과태료`) while enrolled in Police Good Driving Mileage, do I lose all the points I saved in previous years?
**A.** **No!** Banked points from completed prior years **never expire** until you use them. Getting a traffic ticket only voids the current in-progress 12-month pledge cycle, and you can immediately click "Re-Pledge (`재서약`)" on eFine starting the day after the violation.

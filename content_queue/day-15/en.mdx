Under Article 30-2 of the Banking Act of South Korea, borrowers who experience improvements in their creditworthiness (promotions, salary increases, or debt repayment) possess a statutory **Interest Rate Reduction Right (금리인하요구권)** to lower interest rates on existing bank loans.

      <li><strong>Applicable Loans:</strong> Credit lines (minus bankbooks), variable-rate mortgages, and commercial jeonse loans. (Government policy loans excluded).</li>
      <li><strong>Decision Timeline:</strong> Financial institutions must notify approval within 10 business days.</li>

South Korea's **2026 Parental Allowance (부모급여)** program, administered by the Ministry of Health and Welfare (MOHW), provides universal, non-means-tested monthly cash support to every household raising an infant under 24 months of age. Families receive **1,000,000 KRW per month for infants aged 0 (0–11 months)** and **500,000 KRW per month for toddlers aged 1 (12–23 months)**—totaling **18,000,000 KRW over the first two years of life** regardless of household income or net worth.

Even better, Parental Allowance stacks 100% with the **First Meeting Voucher (첫만남이용권, 2M–3M KRW)**, **Child Allowance (아동수당, 100k KRW/mo)**, and Employment Insurance **Parental Leave Benefits (육아휴직급여)**. Here is the complete 2026 benefit matrix, daycare voucher difference calculation, and 60-day retroactive application rule.

## 2. Home Care vs. Daycare Enrollment: How the Cash Difference Works

When you enroll your child in a certified daycare center (어린이집), your Parental Allowance shifts from 100% cash to a **Daycare Tuition Voucher + Cash Difference** structure:
* **Age 0 Daycare Enrollment:** Since the Parental Allowance (1,000,000 KRW) is larger than the standard Age 0 daycare tuition voucher (~540,000 KRW), the government pays the daycare tuition in full via your National Happiness Card (국민행복카드) AND deposits the **~460,000 KRW cash difference** into your bank account on the 20th of the following month!
* **Age 1 Daycare Enrollment:** Because the Age 1 Parental Allowance (500,000 KRW) is close to the Age 1 daycare voucher (~475,000 KRW), the voucher covers daycare tuition and the remaining ~25,000 KRW difference is paid in cash.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Are multicultural families or foreign parents eligible for Parental Allowance?**  
A. If the newborn child holds **South Korean nationality (대한민국 국적)** (including dual citizens registered on the family relation certificate) and resides in Korea with a Resident Registration Number, the child receives 100% of the Parental Allowance, Child Allowance, and First Meeting Voucher even if one parent is a foreign national.

**Q2. What if our child stays abroad for an extended period?**  
A. Under the Child Welfare Act and Parental Allowance guidelines, if a child stays outside of South Korea for **90 consecutive days or longer**, payments are suspended starting the month following the 90th day until the child re-enters Korea.

**Q3. Is Parental Allowance subject to income tax?**  
A. **No.** All government childbirth subsidies, Parental Allowance, and Child Allowance payments are 100% tax-exempt (비과세) and do not count toward your taxable income or health insurance premiums.

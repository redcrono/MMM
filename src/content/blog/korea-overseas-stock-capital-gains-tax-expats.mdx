Foreign residents in South Korea frequently trade US equities (such as Apple, Nvidia, and S&P 500 ETFs) through local Korean brokerages or international platforms.

However, understanding **when you become subject to Korean capital gains tax on overseas assets** is crucial to avoiding severe late-filing penalties.

---

## 1. The Crucial 5-Year Residency Rule for Foreigners

Under the Korean Income Tax Act (Article 118-2), foreigners living in South Korea are classified into two tax categories regarding overseas assets:

## 2. Calculating the 22% Capital Gains Tax

```
[Example: Annual Net Capital Gain of 10,000,000 KRW]
• Total Net Profit: 10,000,000 KRW
• Annual Basic Exemption: -2,500,000 KRW
• Taxable Base: 7,500,000 KRW
• Final Tax (22%): 7,500,000 × 22% = 1,650,000 KRW
```

---

## 3. Annual Filing Deadline (May 1 ~ May 31)

Foreign residents liable for overseas capital gains tax must file their return through **HomeTax** or via an authorized Korean brokerage between **May 1 and May 31** of the following calendar year.

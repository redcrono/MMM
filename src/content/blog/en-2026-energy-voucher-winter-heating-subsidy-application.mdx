To alleviate heavy winter utility expenses for vulnerable households, the Korean government and Korea Energy Agency provide targeted heating subsidies through the **2026 Winter Energy Voucher Program**, distributing up to **₩592,400 per household**.

<div class="smart-brief-box">
  <div class="smart-brief-header">
    <span class="smart-brief-badge">💡 3-Minute Brief</span>
    <span class="smart-brief-time">Core Fact Sheet</span>
  </div>
  <div class="smart-brief-content">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> Basic Living Security Recipients (Livelihood, Medical, Housing, or Education benefit) with qualifying members (seniors 65+, infants, registered disabled persons, or pregnant women).</li>
      <li><strong>Allowance Range:</strong> ₩295,200 for single households up to ₩592,400 for 4+ member households.</li>
      <li><strong>Payment Modes:</strong> Virtual card (automatic utility bill deduction) or Physical Kookmin Happiness Card for kerosene/LPG.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

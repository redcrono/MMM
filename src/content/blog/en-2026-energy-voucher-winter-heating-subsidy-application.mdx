## 1. Dual Qualification Architecture: Income Criteria + Household Characteristic

The South Korean **Energy Voucher (에너지바우처)** is a statutory utility subsidy designed to ensure low-income families can afford electricity, city gas, district heating, kerosene, LPG, and coal briquettes during extreme summer heatwaves and winter freezes. To receive the voucher, a household must simultaneously satisfy **both** the **Income Criterion** and the **Household Member Criterion**:

1. **Income Criterion (National Basic Livelihood Security Act):**
   - Recipient of **Livelihood Benefits (생계급여)**, **Medical Benefits (의료급여)**, **Housing Benefits (주거급여)**, or **Education Benefits (교육급여)**.
2. **Household Member Criterion (Resident Registration Card Basis):**
   - Either the recipient or a registered household member on the same resident registration card must belong to one of the following seven vulnerable groups:
     - **Senior Citizen:** Born on or before December 31, 1961 (Aged 65+).
     - **Infant / Young Child:** Born on or after January 1, 2019 (Aged 7 or under).
     - **Registered Person with Disabilities:** Grades 1–6 (Severe or Mild disability under the Act on Welfare of Persons with Disabilities).
     - **Pregnant Woman:** Currently pregnant or within 6 months after childbirth.
     - **Severe / Rare / Intractable Disease Patient:** Registered under the National Health Insurance Benefit Standards.
     - **Single-Parent Family (한부모가족)** or **Direct-Line Grandparent/Child Household (소년소녀가정)** including foster children.

> **Expat & Multi-Cultural Family Eligibility:** Foreign nationals who are registered on the National Basic Livelihood Security roster (e.g., F-5 Permanent Residents, F-6 Marriage Migrants, or foreign spouses/children forming a legal household with a Korean citizen under Article 5-2 of the National Basic Livelihood Security Act) qualify on the exact same terms.

### Choosing Between the Two Voucher Redemption Formats
When applying, you must choose **one** of two redemption formats for the winter season depending on how your residence is billed for heating:
1. **Virtual Card / Bill Deduction (요금차감 방식):** Best for apartments and multi-family villas where heating (district heating, city gas, or electricity) is billed on a monthly utility invoice or included in the apartment management fee (관리비 고지서). You simply provide your utility customer number (고객번호), and the voucher balance is automatically deducted from your monthly bill.
2. **Physical Kookmin Happiness Card (국민행복카드 실물카드 방식):** Required for households that purchase **heating kerosene (등유), LPG cylinders, or coal briquettes** directly from fuel dealers, or who prefer paying city gas/electricity bills manually via card terminal or phone.

## 3. Stacking Strategy: Combine Energy Voucher + City Gas Welfare Discount

A widespread misconception is that receiving the Energy Voucher disqualifies you from other utility discounts. In reality, you can **stack (중복 수혜)** the Energy Voucher on top of:
- **KEPCO Electricity Welfare Discount (한전 전기요금 복지할인):** Up to ₩16,000–₩20,000/month off electricity bills for basic livelihood and disability households.
- **City Gas Social Welfare Discount (도시가스 요금경감):** Up to **₩148,000/month winter discount** applied directly by regional city gas providers (Seoul City Gas, Samchully, etc.). Applying for the City Gas discount first and then using your Energy Voucher to pay the remaining balance eliminates nearly 100% of winter heating out-of-pocket costs!

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Why did my automatic enrollment fail after I moved to a new house?
Automatic re-enrollment is immediately paused whenever a recipient **changes their residential address (전입신고)** or experiences a change in household composition, because your utility customer number and heating fuel type at the new address have changed. You **must** visit your new neighborhood Community Service Center or log into Bokjiro to submit an **Information Change & Re-Application (정보변경 신청)** so your remaining balance links to your new utility meter!

### Q2. Can I switch from a Virtual Card (Bill Deduction) to a Physical Card mid-winter?
**Yes.** If you initially chose Bill Deduction for city gas but later need to buy kerosene for a portable heater, you can switch your voucher delivery method online via `bokjiro.go.kr` or at your Community Service Center during the winter application/change window.

### Q3. Can I receive the Energy Voucher if I already received a Coal Briquette Coupon (연탄쿠폰) or Kerosene Voucher (등유바우처)?
**No.** While you can stack the Energy Voucher with KEPCO electricity discounts and City Gas welfare rate reductions, statutory rules prohibit overlapping receipt with the **Winter Kerosene Voucher (등유바우처)** or **Coal Briquette Coupon (연탄쿠폰)**. Compare the total monetary value of each program and select the higher subsidy.

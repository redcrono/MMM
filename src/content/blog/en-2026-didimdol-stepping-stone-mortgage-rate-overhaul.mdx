South Korea's **Didimdol Loan (내집마련 디딤돌대출)**, administered by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT) and the Korea Housing Finance Corporation (HF), remains the most powerful government-subsidized mortgage program for non-homeowners. Offering fixed or 5-year periodic interest rates **1.5%p to 2.0%p below commercial bank mortgages**, it can save a household over 30 million KRW in interest over a 30-year term.

However, 2026 policy updates to preferential rate caps, regional small-tenant deposit deductions (**방공제**), and net asset verification rules require precise financial planning before signing a real estate purchase contract. Here is the complete 2026 Didimdol Mortgage guide.

## 2. How to Stack Preferential Discounts Down to 1.5%~2.15%

Base rates for Didimdol range between **2.45% and 3.55% APR** depending on income bracket and maturity (10, 15, 20, or 30 years). You can slash your rate by combining these official discounts:

1. **Housing Subscription Savings Account (청약저축) Discount (Up to -0.5%p):**
   * 5+ years & 60+ monthly installments: **-0.3%p**
   * 10+ years & 120+ installments: **-0.4%p**
   * 15+ years & 180+ installments: **-0.5%p**
2. **Real Estate Electronic Contract System (부동산 전자계약) (-0.1%p):**
   * Asking your licensed realtor to execute the purchase agreement via MOLIT's digital contract system (`irts.molit.go.kr`) automatically shaves **0.1%p** off your mortgage rate for the entire 30-year life of the loan.
3. **Household & Demographic Discounts:**
   * 3+ children (**-0.7%p**), 2 children (**-0.5%p**), 1 child (**-0.3%p**), Newlywed (**-0.2%p**), First-time buyer (**-0.2%p**).
   * Additionally, if you give birth to a child *after* loan origination, each newborn triggers an additional **-0.2%p rate reduction** on your existing balance.

## 3. Crucial Underwriting Pitfall: Small-Tenant Deposit Deduction (방공제)

Under the Housing Lease Protection Act, lenders must reserve a statutory priority deposit amount per room—**55M KRW in Seoul, 48M KRW in the Greater Seoul Overcrowding Control Zone, and 25M–28M KRW in regional cities**.
* If Mortgage Credit Guarantee (MCG) insurance is restricted for your region/property type, your effective max loan is calculated as `(Property Price × LTV) - 55M KRW`. Always verify MCG eligibility with your handling bank (Woori, KB, Shinhan, Hana, NH) before paying your non-refundable 10% contract deposit.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Can I combine Didimdol with Bogeumjari Loan if the Didimdol cap isn't enough?**  
A. **Yes!** If your property qualifies and your LTV/DTI ratios allow higher borrowing than the Didimdol cap (e.g., 250M KRW), you can simultaneously apply for a secondary **Bogeumjari Loan (보금자리론)** to cover the remaining LTV balance.

**Q2. What happens if our combined income exceeds 60M/85M KRW next year after getting the loan?**  
A. Eligibility is assessed strictly at the **time of loan application**. Even if your household income doubles after loan disbursement, your Didimdol loan and preferential interest rate remain 100% intact until maturity.

**Q3. Is there an early repayment penalty (중도상환수수료) if I pay off the loan early?**  
A. A sliding prepayment fee of up to **1.2%** applies only during the first 3 years from origination. Once **36 months (3 years)** have elapsed, prepayment penalties drop to **0 KRW**, allowing you to make lump-sum principal payments completely free of charge.

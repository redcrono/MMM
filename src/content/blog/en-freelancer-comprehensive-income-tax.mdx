For independent contractors, software developers, designers, English instructors, content creators, and gig workers in South Korea whose earnings are subject to **3.3% Business Income Withholding Tax (3% National + 0.3% Local)**, every May is the statutory **Comprehensive Income Tax (종합소득세) Filing Month**.

Many expat contractors mistakenly believe the 3.3% withheld at source is a final settlement.

In reality, blindly clicking the automated estimation option on mobile NTS notices can trigger **millions of won in surprise tax assessments alongside immediate disqualification from family health insurance dependent coverage (건강보험 피부양자 탈락)**.

This master guide details the 24M KRW expense ratio threshold, Type D vs. Type F notices, simplified bookkeeping deduction strategies, and social insurance protection protocols.

## 4. Health Insurance Dependent Coverage Defense Rules

Expat contractors covered under a spouse or parent's **National Health Insurance (NHIS) Dependent Status (피부양자)** must observe strict net income caps:

- **Unregistered Freelancers (No Tax ID)**: Net annual business profit (Revenue - Expenses) must remain **strictly at or below 5,000,000 KRW** to retain dependent status.
- **Registered Sole Proprietorships (사업자등록)**: **Any net profit exceeding 0 KRW** instantly terminates dependent coverage, triggering independent regional health insurance billing.
- Simplified bookkeeping in May is vital to document legitimate business expenses and keep net income under the 5M KRW threshold.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. I work a full-time job and have 3.3% freelance side income. Will my employer find out?
**No.** Complete your standard Year-End Settlement with your employer in January. In May, log into NTS HomeTax independently and merge your employment withholding statement with your 3.3% side revenue. NTS does not disclose side filings to employers unless secondary income exceeds 20M KRW annually (triggering separate NHIS billing).

### Q2. Can foreign resident freelancers subscribe to Yellow Umbrella Deductions (노란우산공제)?
**Yes.** Registered sole proprietors and 3.3% freelancers holding an ARC can contribute to the Yellow Umbrella Mutual Aid Fund, capturing up to **5,000,000 KRW in direct income deductions from comprehensive income**.

### Q3. Should I use paid refund apps or file directly on HomeTax?
For Type F/G simple ratio taxpayers, NTS HomeTax / SonTax mobile app provides a 100% free, 1-click **"Pre-Filled All-Fill (모두채움)"** refund process. Avoiding third-party platforms with 10%–20% commission fees ensures you keep 100% of your refund.

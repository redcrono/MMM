When paying off debts early with lump-sum cash or refinancing high-interest bank debt to lower-rate lenders in South Korea, the primary financial friction is the <strong>Prepayment Penalty (중도상환수수료), typically levied at 0.5% to 1.4% of outstanding principal</strong>.

On a 300 Million KRW apartment mortgage, early repayment within the first year can trigger <strong>over 2.4 Million KRW in termination penalties</strong>.

However, under Article 20 of the Financial Consumer Protection Act, all bank loans are subject to a <strong>mandatory 3-year (1,095 days) automatic fee waiver</strong>, pro-rata daily deductions, and 2026 FSC actual-cost reforms.

This master guide details the pro-rata calculation formula, Break-Even Point (BEP) refinancing analytics, and 100% fee-exempt loan types.

## 3. 5 Loan Scenarios with 100% Prepayment Penalty Waivers

1. <strong>Vulnerable / Policy Borrowers</strong>: Borrowers in the bottom 30% credit bracket or utilizing state policy loans (Sunshine Loan, Saeheeman Seed) receive 100% fee waivers.
2. <strong>Repayment within 1 Month of Maturity</strong>: Most commercial banks waive all fees if repaid within 30 days of standard loan expiration.
3. <strong>Overdraft Lines (마이너스통장)</strong>: Demand revolving facilities carry strictly <strong>0 KRW prepayment fees at all times</strong>.
4. <strong>Bank-Initiated Rate Hikes</strong>: If the bank increases rates due to internal changes and the borrower terminates the contract.
5. <strong>Involuntary Circumstances</strong>: Property eminent domain, forced auction, or borrower death.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. What if I make a partial principal prepayment of 10,000,000 KRW?
Penalties are assessed <strong>strictly against the specific 10M KRW being prepaid</strong>, not the total loan balance. Making periodic partial prepayments reduces cumulative compounding interest substantially.

### Q2. What changed under the 2026 FSC Prepayment Fee Reform?
Banks are legally prohibited from charging flat 1.2%–1.4% margins, restricting penalties to <strong>actual incurred administrative costs (appraisal fees, stamp taxes, agent commissions), cutting real fees to 0.5%–0.7%</strong>.

### Q3. Can foreign residents refinance Korean mortgages online?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Registered foreign nationals with digital certificates and an Alien Registration Card (ARC) can utilize the Korea Financial Telecommunications loan-switching infrastructure to refinance mortgages seamlessly.

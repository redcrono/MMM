The Financial Services Commission (FSC) has officially eased the stringent 5-year lockup period of the **Youth Jump Account (청년도약계좌)**. 

Beginning in 2026, participants aged 19 to 34 who maintain the account for at least **3 years** are permitted to exit with **100% tax-exempt interest treatment and 60% of accrued government matching subsidies**.

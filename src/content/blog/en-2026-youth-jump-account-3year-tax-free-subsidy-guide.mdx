## 1. Why the 3-Year Rule Change Transforms Youth Asset Building

When the South Korean government first launched the **Youth Jump Account (청년도약계좌)**—a 5-year policy savings program allowing young adults aged 19–34 to deposit up to ₩700,000 per month to build a ₩50,000,000 nest egg—its biggest weakness was liquidity lock-in. Previously, if a subscriber needed cash in Year 3 or Year 4 for a wedding, Jeonse deposit, or tuition and canceled the account outside strict statutory hardship exceptions, the government clawed back 100% of its matching grants, revoked the 15.4% tax-free status, and slashed the bank interest rate to ~1.0%.

Under the **3-Year Holding Reform**, any subscriber who maintains the account for **36 months or longer** can terminate at will for any personal reason and still lock in:
1. **Full 15.4% Tax-Free Exemption (비과세 유지)** on all interest and subsidies earned.
2. **60% of Accrued Government Contributions (정부기여금 60% 지급)**.
3. **Upgraded Bank Early-Termination Rate of 3.8%–4.5% p.a.** (matching commercial 3-year time deposit rates), resulting in an effective 3-year yield of up to **7.6% p.a.**

Under the revised structure, **every eligible income tier receives an extra 3.0% matching contribution on deposits up to the full ₩700,000 monthly cap**:

## 3. Two Liquidity Lifelines: 40% Partial Withdrawal & Special Mid-Term Termination

Before closing your account at the 3-year mark, check whether one of these two superior options applies to your situation:
1. **40% Partial Withdrawal Service (부분인출서비스):**
   Once you have held the account for **2 years (24 months)** or longer, you can make a one-time partial withdrawal of **up to 40% of your accumulated deposit principal** directly inside your banking app. The remaining 60% continues compounding toward the full 5-year 6.0% maturity rate and 100% government subsidy!
2. **100% Subsidy Preservation via Special Mid-Term Termination (특별중도해지):**
   If you terminate early due to **marriage, childbirth, first-time home purchase (생애최초 주택구입)**, retirement, business closure, or death/disability, you do **not** suffer the 40% subsidy haircut of a standard 3-year withdrawal. Submitting your Marriage Relation Certificate, Childbirth Certificate, or Home Purchase Contract within 6 months of the event entitles you to **100% of the government subsidy and the full 6.0% contract interest rate** regardless of how many months you held the account.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. If I already opened my Youth Jump Account in 2023 or 2024, do I automatically get the expanded government matching and 3-year rule?
**Yes, 100% automatically!** Both the expanded monthly government contribution ceiling (up to ₩33,000/month) and the 3-year early withdrawal tax-free/60%-subsidy benefit apply retroactively and automatically to all existing Youth Jump Account holders without requiring re-enrollment.

### Q2. What happens if my salary rises above ₩75,000,000 after I open the account?
Your account is **never canceled**, and your **15.4% tax-free interest exemption is permanently locked in** for the full 5 years based on your initial enrollment eligibility. The Korea Inclusive Finance Agency (KINFA) recalculates your personal income tier once a year solely to adjust your monthly government matching grant rate for the upcoming 12 months.

### Q3. Can I transfer my maturing Youth Hope Savings (청년희망적금) lump sum into this account?
**Yes.** Through the official **Lump-Sum Payment (일시납입)** program, maturing Youth Hope Savings funds (up to ₩12,600,000) can be deposited upfront as an 18-month prepaid block, immediately earning compound interest and government matching from Day 1.

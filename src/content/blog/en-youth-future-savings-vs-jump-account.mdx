For young adults building their initial 50,000,000 KRW seed capital in South Korea, the Ministry of Economy and Finance and FSC offer the <strong>Youth Leap Account (청년도약계좌)</strong> under Article 91-22 of the Restriction of Special Taxation Act.

Depositing up to 700,000 KRW monthly over a 5-year horizon combines <strong>6.0% high-yield bank deposit rates + monthly government matching grants (up to ~390,000 KRW/yr) + 100% tax exemption on interest</strong>, generating an effective return equivalent to a <strong>9.54% standard commercial bank savings deposit</strong>.

This master guide details 2026 eligibility standards, payout simulations by salary bracket, and early exit protections.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents holding an ARC open a Youth Leap Account?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Foreign residents aged 19–34 who have filed Korean income taxes (근로소득 or 종합소득세) for the preceding tax year and qualify as domestic tax residents under the Income Tax Act are eligible.

### Q2. Does receiving a salary raise above 75 Million KRW disqualify existing accounts?
<strong>No.</strong> Income eligibility is audited strictly at the time of initial enrollment; subsequent salary raises do not impact your tax-free status or grant accumulation.

### Q3. Can individuals on maternity or childcare leave participate?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Under statutory special rules, individuals receiving statutory childcare leave allowances (육아휴직급여) during the preceding tax period are fully eligible.

When visiting or living in South Korea, cash is rarely needed because almost all merchants, cafes, taxis, and subways accept digital cards.

However, using foreign credit cards frequently incurs a **3% to 5% foreign transaction and currency conversion markup fee**.

To travel and shop smart, choosing the right prepaid travel and transit card is your best money-saving move!

## 3. Instant Incheon Airport Tax Refund Tip

Remember that when purchasing goods over 15,000 KRW at tax-free participating stores in Korea, you can claim an **instant 7% ~ 10% VAT tax refund** directly at airport customs tax refund kiosks before boarding your flight!

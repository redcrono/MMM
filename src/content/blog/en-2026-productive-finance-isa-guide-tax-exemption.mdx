Starting September 1, 2026, South Korea's financial authorities officially launch the <strong>'Productive Finance ISA' (생산적 금융 ISA)</strong>—a new tier of Individual Savings Account offering expanded tax exemptions to bolster domestic capital market participation.

The cornerstone upgrades include <strong>doubling the dividend and interest tax exemption ceiling</strong> for portfolios maintaining at least 50% allocation in domestic listed equities and mutual funds, alongside a <strong>brand-new 10% annual income tax deduction for youth contributors aged 19 to 34</strong>.

Whether you currently manage an existing Brokerage ISA or are planning to open your first tax-advantaged account, understanding these regulatory enhancements can unlock millions in statutory tax savings.

This comprehensive guide breaks down the core architecture of the Productive Finance ISA, contrasts it against standard Brokerage ISAs, models youth tax deductions, and details a 3-step asset allocation roadmap.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. How can I switch my existing Brokerage ISA to a Productive ISA?
Starting September 1, investors can seamlessly convert existing accounts via their brokerage mobile apps (MTS) under the "ISA Account Settings" menu without liquidating holdings.

### Q2. Does the one-account-per-person rule still apply?
Yes. Investors may hold only <strong>one active ISA account</strong> across all financial institutions. You must choose between the Standard Brokerage ISA and the Productive Finance ISA based on your asset allocation strategy.

### Q3. What happens if domestic equity allocation falls below 50%?
A grace period is provided for market fluctuations. However, prolonged non-compliance across quarterly reviews may revert your exemption cap back to standard ₩2M–₩4M tiers.

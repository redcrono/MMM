For young working professionals, graduate students, and university attendees living independently in South Korea, monthly housing rent represents their single largest recurring financial burden.

Administered by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT), the <strong>Youth Monthly Rent Special Support Program (청년 월세 특별지원)</strong> deposits <strong>200,000 KRW per month in direct, non-taxable cash subsidies for up to 12 months (totaling 2,400,000 KRW)</strong> directly into qualifying applicants' bank accounts.

This master guide evaluates 2026 median income thresholds, parental income audit exemption clauses, housing qualification caps, and the step-by-step Bokjiro application protocol.

## 2. 2026 Statutory Median Income Thresholds

| Household Size | Youth Household (≤ 60% Median) | Parental Household (≤ 100% Median) |
|:---|:---:|:---:|
| <strong>1-Person Household</strong> | <strong>≤ 1,450,000 KRW / month</strong> | - |
| <strong>2-Person Household</strong> | ≤ 2,360,000 KRW / month | ≤ 3,930,000 KRW / month |
| <strong>3-Person Household</strong> | ≤ 3,020,000 KRW / month | <strong>≤ 5,030,000 KRW / month</strong> |
| <strong>4-Person Household</strong> | ≤ 3,680,000 KRW / month | <strong>≤ 6,130,000 KRW / month</strong> |

### 💡 3 Key Exceptions: Waiving Parental Income Audits
If you meet any of the following 3 criteria, parental income and assets (470M KRW cap) are <strong>completely waived</strong>, evaluating only the <strong>youth applicant's independent income and assets (≤ 122M KRW)</strong>:
1. Youth aged <strong>30 or older</strong>.
2. <strong>Legally married</strong> or divorced youth.
3. Youth under 30 who earn <strong>at least 50% of the standard median income (~1.21M KRW/month)</strong> in verifiable continuous earnings.

## 3. Payout Schedule & Direct Cash Disbursements

- <strong>Monthly Grant</strong>: <strong>Up to 200,000 KRW per month</strong> (Actual rent paid, excluding maintenance maintenance fees).
- <strong>Duration</strong>: Once in a lifetime, disbursed for <strong>up to 12 consecutive months (Total 2,400,000 KRW)</strong>.
- <strong>Disbursement Date</strong>: Wired on the <strong>25th of each calendar month</strong> directly to the applicant's checking account.
- <strong>Tax Status</strong>: 100% tax-free government welfare grant; zero impact on income taxes or health insurance premiums.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. If I move to a new apartment mid-grant, do my payments terminate?
<strong>No.</strong> After moving, register your new address at the local community center within 14 days and submit an <strong>Address Change Form with your new lease contract on Bokjiro</strong>. Remaining grant installments will seamlessly transition to your new home.

### Q2. Are residents of LH/SH public housing or Happy Housing eligible?
<strong>No.</strong> Public rental housing (LH/SH Happy Housing, Station-Area Youth Housing) already receives extensive government housing subsidies and is statutorily excluded. Only private market studio, officetel, and villa rentals qualify.

### Q3. Can foreign residents and multicultural family youth apply?
Foreign nationals holding permanent residency (F-5 visa) or married to Korean nationals (F-6 visa) who maintain separate household registration and satisfy income thresholds are <strong>fully eligible to receive the 2.4M KRW cash grant</strong>.

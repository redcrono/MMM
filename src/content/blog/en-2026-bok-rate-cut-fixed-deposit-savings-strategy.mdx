Following the US Federal Reserve's rate easing cycle, the **Bank of Korea (BOK)** is widely expected to initiate policy rate cuts. 

When base rates fall, commercial banks immediately slash deposit interest rates by 25 to 50 basis points. For expats and domestic savers in South Korea, the remaining weeks before the rate decision represent the critical window to lock in multi-year fixed yields.

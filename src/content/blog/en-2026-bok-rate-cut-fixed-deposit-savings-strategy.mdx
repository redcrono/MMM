## 2. 3 Institutional-Grade Strategies to Beat Falling Deposit Rates

### ① The 40-30-30 Split Deposit Rule (Emergency Liquidity Shield)
If you lock 100 million KRW into a single 2-year deposit and unexpectedly need 25 million KRW for a medical or housing expense in month 14, breaking the deposit forces the bank to apply a punitive **early termination rate (중도해지이율, often 0.5%–1.0%)** across the entire 100 million KRW.
* **Pro Solution:** Divide your principal into three separate fixed deposit contracts (40M + 30M + 30M) or enable the bank's **"Partial Withdrawal (일부해지, up to 2–3 times)"** feature at origination.

### ② Mutual Finance Membership Tax Shield (세금우대 저축)
Any Korean resident aged 19 or older can join a local Credit Union (신협), Saemaul Geumgo (새마을금고), or Regional NongHyup (단위농협) as a cooperative member (조합원/준조합원) by depositing a refundable membership share (출자금, typically 10,000 to 50,000 KRW). This unlocks a unified **30 million KRW tax-preferred quota** taxed at only **1.4%** instead of 15.4%.

### ③ Guarding Against the 20M KRW Financial Income Tax Cliff
If your combined annual interest and dividend income exceeds **20 million KRW**, you are subject to Comprehensive Financial Income Taxation (up to 49.5% marginal rate) and lose dependent status under National Health Insurance. Choosing **monthly interest payout (월이자지급식)** deposits instead of maturity-lump-sum payouts prevents multiple years of interest from clustering into a single tax year.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Are deposits at Credit Unions (신협) and Saemaul Geumgo protected up to 50 million KRW?**  
A. Yes. While commercial banks and savings banks are insured by the Korea Deposit Insurance Corporation (KDIC), Credit Unions, Saemaul Geumgo, and NongHyup operate their own statutory Depositor Protection Funds instituted by federal law, guaranteeing **up to 50 million KRW (principal + interest) per person** at each federation/safe.

**Q2. Is it better to choose Simple Interest (단리) or Monthly Compounding (월복리)?**  
A. At identical headline APRs, **Monthly Compounding (월복리)** yields roughly 0.06%p to 0.08%p more over a 12-to-24-month period because each month's interest earns additional interest in subsequent months.

**Q3. Can I take out a loan against my fixed deposit instead of breaking it?**  
A. Yes! If you need short-term cash within 1–2 months of maturity, use a **Deposit-Secured Overdraft Loan (예담대/예금담보대출)**. Banks lend up to 95% of your deposit balance at `Deposit Rate + 1.0~1.25%p`, which is almost always far cheaper than forfeiting 10+ months of accrued fixed deposit interest.

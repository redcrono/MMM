## 1. Escaping the High-Interest Debt Spiral for Small Business Owners

Across South Korea, self-employed entrepreneurs and micro-enterprise owners (소상공인) who took out working-capital loans from savings banks (저축은행), capital companies (캐피탈), credit card firms, or high-spread commercial banks face punishing interest rates of **7% to 15% p.a.** Not only do these loans drain monthly operating cash flow, but holding non-bank high-interest debt drags down NICE and KCB personal credit scores by 40 to 90 points.

To prevent viable small businesses from collapsing under debt service costs, the Ministry of SMEs and Startups (중소벤처기업부) and the Small Enterprise and Market Service (소상공인시장진흥공단 - SEMAS) operate a three-pillar refinancing architecture that cuts interest expenses in half while extending repayment schedules up to **10 years**.

> **`Annual Interest Saved (₩50M Debt at 11.5% → 4.5%) = ₩50,000,000 × (11.5% - 4.5%) = ₩3,500,000 / year`**

## 3. Core Eligibility Criteria for the 4.5% SEMAS Policy Refinance Loan

To qualify for the flagship **4.5% fixed-rate SEMAS Policy Refinance Loan**, your business and existing debt must satisfy four strict checkpoints:
1. **Small Business Status (소상공인 기준):** Annual sales within SME sector thresholds and fewer than **5 full-time employees** (fewer than 10 employees for manufacturing, mining, construction, and transportation).
2. **Credit Score Requirement:** Personal credit score of **NCB (NICE) 839 or lower** (low-to-mid credit track), or business viability verification if applying under the general track.
3. **Eligible Target Loans:** Business loans (사업자대출)—including bank credit loans, savings bank loans, and card/capital business loans—carrying a current interest rate of **7.0% p.a. or higher**. (Note: Real estate purchase mortgages and policy funds already issued by SEMAS or regional credit guarantee foundations are excluded.)
4. **Zero Current Delinquency:** You must have **no active tax delinquency** (national or local taxes) and no active loan overdue records at the moment of application. If you have minor overdue tax balances, pay them off on HomeTax/WeTax at least 3 business days before applying!

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can I refinance a personal credit loan (가계신용대출) that I used to pay my store rent and inventory?
Under the **SEMAS 4.5% Policy Loan**, only loans officially registered as **Business Loans (사업자대출)** are eligible. However, under the **KODIT Low-Rate Refinance Guarantee (저금리 대환보증)** at commercial banks, up to **₩20,000,000 of personal household credit loans or card loans** can be refinanced into a 5.0%–5.5% business guarantee loan if you submit receipts (tax invoices, rent transfers, or supplier statements) proving the funds were used for business operations.

### Q2. Are there early repayment fees (중도상환수수료) when paying off my old 7%+ loan?
Under Financial Services Commission guidelines, major commercial banks and savings banks **waive early repayment penalties** when a small business owner pays off a high-interest loan using a government policy refinance loan. Furthermore, the new 4.5% SEMAS refinance loan carries **zero early repayment penalty**, allowing you to pay down principal whenever your store revenue rebounds.

### Q3. Why does refinancing from a savings bank to a partner commercial bank raise my credit score?
Korean credit bureaus (KCB and NICE) heavily penalize the **institution tier (업권별 가중치)** of your debt. Replacing a 12% savings bank or capital company loan with a 4.5% Tier-1 bank policy loan removes the non-bank borrower flag, typically boosting personal credit scores by **30 to 65 points** within 30 days.

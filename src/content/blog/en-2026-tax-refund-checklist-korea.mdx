If you work for a company in South Korea, every January brings the country's most important personal tax event: **Yeonmaljeongsan (Year-End Tax Settlement)**.

Commonly celebrated as the **"13th Month Salary"**, filing your deductions correctly can return over 1,000,000 KRW to 3,000,000 KRW directly to your bank account in February.

## 2. Card Spending Strategy: Credit vs. Debit Cards

You only receive tax deductions on card spending that **exceeds 25% of your total gross annual salary**:

| Payment Method | Deduction Rate | Optimal Usage Strategy |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Credit Card** | **15%** | Use up to the 25% salary threshold for reward points |
| **Check (Debit) Card / Cash Receipt** | **30% (Double!)** | Use exclusively once 25% threshold is surpassed |
| **Traditional Markets and Public Transit** | **40% ~ 80%** | Additional 1,000,000 KRW dedicated deduction ceiling |

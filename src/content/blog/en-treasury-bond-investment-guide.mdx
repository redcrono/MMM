## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. What happens if I redeem Korean Retail Treasury Bonds early?
Early redemption is permitted <strong>after 1 year from issuance</strong>. However, early redemption forfeits the add-on rate and compounding—paying only simple baseline coupon interest—and <strong>cancels the 14% separate tax privilege, reverting to standard taxable income</strong>.

### Q2. How are subscriptions allocated during high demand?
The government enforces a <strong>Small Investor Priority Rule (소액 우선 배정)</strong>, ensuring all orders up to 3,000,000 KRW are filled equally before prorating remaining supplies.

### Q3. Can foreign residents open dedicated Retail Treasury Bond accounts?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Any foreign national who is a registered tax resident in South Korea holding an Alien Registration Card (ARC) can open a dedicated Treasury Bond account through the designated agent broker (Mirae Asset Securities) and participate in monthly public offerings.

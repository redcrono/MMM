## 1. The September 2026 Fed Pivot and 'Duration-17' Mechanics

However, the magnitude of this price surge depends entirely on the bond's maturity, measured by <strong>Duration (interest rate sensitivity)</strong>.

### The Mathematical Formula for Bond Price Movement
> 📌 **Bond Price Change (%) ≈ -Duration × Yield Change (%p)**

With an effective duration of roughly **16.5 to 17.5 years**, if 30-year US Treasury yields retreat by **1.0%p (100 bps) from 4.4% to 3.4%, the underlying bond ETF surges by approximately +17%**. This offers stock-like returns with sovereign government backing.

Typically, <strong>Fed interest rate cuts weaken the US dollar, causing USD/KRW to fall (won strengthens)</strong>. What happens to your returns if you hold an unhedged ETF like TLT during an exchange rate drop?

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Could 30-year Treasury yields fail to drop even after the Fed cuts rates?
**Yes, temporarily.** The Fed controls the Federal Funds Rate (ultra-short-term rate), but 30-year yields reflect long-term inflation expectations and the volume of US government debt issuance. If massive fiscal deficits flood the market with bond supply, long-term yields may lag short-term rate cuts. Dollar-cost averaging across 3 to 6 months mitigates this timing risk.

### Q2. Do Korea-listed Treasury ETFs match the performance of US-listed TLT?
**Yes, the underlying asset performance is identical.** Products like ACE US 30-Year Treasury track physical US long-term bonds, capturing the same price movements as TLT. Furthermore, Korea-listed products offer **currency-hedged options, eligibility for ISA and Retirement Pension (DC/IRP) accounts, and significant tax advantages**.

### Q3. What is the outlook for 3x leveraged bond ETFs like TMF?
**Strongly discouraged for long-term holds.** Direxion Daily 20+ Year Treasury Bull 3X (TMF) is rebalanced daily. Any volatility, chop, or yield pauses trigger severe 'Volatility Drag' (beta decay), eroding your principal even if yields eventually drop. Stick to 1x unleveraged ETFs for sustained multi-quarter easing cycles.

### Q4. Must I hold long-term Treasury ETFs until maturity in my retirement account?
**No, absolutely not.** ETFs are publicly traded exchange funds. Whenever bond yields drop and you achieve your target capital gain (e.g., +15% to +25%), you can sell your shares instantly during trading hours and reallocate your capital into equity index ETFs or high-yield cash equivalents.

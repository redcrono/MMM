When purchasing a primary residence or financing a Jeonse apartment security deposit in South Korea, receiving financial support from family members is common practice.

However, with the National Tax Service (NTS) aggressively leveraging **Property & Consumption Analysis Systems (PCI)** and **Post-Debt Automated Audits**, transfers between parents and children lacking a legally sound loan agreement and verifiable repayment traces are **instantly classified as gifts—triggering substantial gift tax bills plus 20%+ failure-to-report penalties**.

This master guide breaks down Article 41-4 of the Korean Inheritance and Gift Tax Act, detailing the statutory **217 Million KRW interest-free borrowing limit, essential contract clauses, and the 600 KRW electronic certified date notarization process**.

## 2. 6 Mandatory Clauses to Pass NTS Tax Audits

A written contract alone is insufficient; the NTS evaluates **whether the borrower genuinely possesses the capacity and intent to repay**.

1. **Party Identification**: Full legal names, Resident Registration Numbers (ARC numbers for foreigners), residential addresses, and contact details for both lender (parent) and borrower (child).
2. **Principal & Execution Date**: Exact sum borrowed and the exact banking execution date.
3. **Interest Terms & Due Dates**: For 0% loans, state *"0% interest pursuant to Article 41-4 of the Inheritance and Gift Tax Act"*. For interest-bearing loans, state *"4.6% annual interest payable on the 25th of each month"*.
4. **Maturity & Repayment Method**: Realistic term (3 to 5 years recommended) and amortization schedule (bullet maturity or monthly principal amortizations).
5. **Acceleration Clause (기한이익 상실)**: Immediate call of full balance in the event of default.
6. **Execution & Signature**: Handwritten physical signature or registered personal seal stamp (인감도장).

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Is a loan agreement with an unemployed college student or minor valid?
**No.** If the borrower lacks documented employment or business income, the NTS disregards the loan agreement as an artificial sham and **taxes the entire principal as a gift with penalties**. The borrower must have verifiable income to service the loan.

### Q2. Does the parent need to report taxes on interest received?
**Statutorily, non-business loan interest is subject to 27.5% withholding tax (25% national + 2.5% local).** While borrowers technically withhold and remit this by the 10th of the following month, parents generally declare interest income during their comprehensive May tax filing.

### Q3. Can we set a 30-year maturity term for family loans?
**Extremely risky.** Setting a 30-year term for an elderly parent (e.g., in their 70s) is viewed by tax auditors as an implicit evasion of gift tax. Structure agreements in **3- to 5-year terms and formally re-execute renewal contracts if needed**.

Every November and December, tax accountants across South Korea recommend one universal "no-risk, 30% guaranteed return" tax hack before the December 31 deadline: the **Hometown Love Donation System (`고향사랑기부제`, Gohyang Sarang Donation)**. Unlike pension savings (`연금저축`) or IRP accounts that lock up your capital until age 55, or credit card deductions that require spending over 25% of your gross salary first, this program works immediately with just **100,000 KRW**.

When you donate **100,000 KRW** to any local government in Korea outside your current registered municipality, the National Tax Service (NTS) and your local tax office reduce your payable income tax by **100,000 KRW (100%)**, and the recipient municipality immediately rewards you with **30,000 points (30% of your donation)** to order premium Korean beef (`한우`), Jeju tangerines, 10kg bags of新rice, or mobile local gift certificates.

In this 2026 master guide, we break down **the exact tiered tax credit formulas, the 2026 44% mid-tier expansion, address eligibility rules, the Determined Tax (`결정세액`) trap, and the 4-step online application process**.

## 1. How the 2026 Hometown Love Donation Tax Credit Formula Works

Under Article 58 of the Restriction of Special Taxation Act, any individual taxpayer (including foreign residents subject to comprehensive year-end tax settlement under the standard progressive rate, though excluding those opting for the 19% flat expat tax rate which forfeits all tax credits) can donate up to **20,000,000 KRW per year** to municipalities outside their registered address.

The combined tax credit (National Income Tax Credit + 10% Local Income Tax automatic reduction) follows a tiered bracket structure:

> **`[2026 Hometown Love Donation Tiered Tax Credit Formula]`**
> * **Up to 100,000 KRW:** Donation × **100%** (100/110 National Income Tax + 10/110 Local Income Tax = **100,000 KRW full refund**)
> * **100,001 KRW ～ 200,000 KRW (2026 Enhanced Bracket):** 100,000 KRW + (Amount over 100,000 KRW × **44%**) *(40% National + 4% Local)*
> * **Over 200,000 KRW ～ 20,000,000 KRW (General Municipality):** 144,000 KRW + (Amount over 200,000 KRW × **16.5%**) *(15% National + 1.5% Local)*
> * **Over 200,000 KRW ～ 20,000,000 KRW (Special Disaster Zone):** 144,000 KRW + (Amount over 200,000 KRW × **33%**) *(30% National + 3% Local, when donated within 3 months of disaster declaration)*

Why does the tax law state `100/110` (approx. 90.91%) for the first 100,000 KRW? Because in Korea, Local Income Tax (`지방소득세`) is automatically assessed at exactly 10% of your National Income Tax (`소득세`). Reducing your National Income Tax by 90,909 KRW automatically reduces your Local Income Tax by 9,091 KRW—totaling **100,000 KRW (100%)**.

## 2. Address Restriction Rules: Where Can You Donate?

Despite its name ("Hometown Love"), **you do NOT need any birth or family connection to a municipality to donate to it**. The only legal rule is that you cannot donate to the local government where your current resident registration (`주민등록지` / `체류지`) is located.

1. **If your registered address is in Gangnam-gu, Seoul (`서울특별시 강남구`):**
   - **Prohibited:** Seoul Metropolitan Government (`서울특별시 본청`) and Gangnam-gu (`강남구`).
   - **Permitted:** Any other district in Seoul (e.g., Seocho-gu, Mapo-gu, Songpa-gu) AND any other province, city, or county in Korea (Gyeonggi-do, Gangwon-do, Jeju-do, Busan, etc.).
2. **If your registered address is in Seongnam-si, Gyeonggi-do (`경기도 성남시`):**
   - **Prohibited:** Gyeonggi Provincial Government (`경기도 본청`) and Seongnam-si (`성남시`).
   - **Permitted:** Any other city/county in Gyeonggi-do (e.g., Suwon-si, Yongin-si, Gapyeong-gun) and all other provinces/cities nationwide.

## 3. Three Critical Rules Before You Donate

### ① Check Your "Determined Tax (`결정세액`)" First
The 100,000 KRW tax credit is **non-refundable** (`비환급성 세액공제`) and cannot be carried forward to future tax years.
- If your gross salary is low or your existing deductions already reduce your **Determined Tax (Line 74 on your Year-End Tax Settlement Receipt, `결정세액`) to 0 KRW**, donating 100,000 KRW will NOT generate a 100,000 KRW cash refund.
- Always verify on NTS Hometax that your estimated annual Determined Tax is **at least 100,000 KRW**.

### ② Dual-Income Couples Should Donate 100,000 KRW Each (No Dependent Pooling)
Unlike standard charitable donations, **Hometown Love Donations cannot be claimed by a spouse or head of household on behalf of a dependent**. Only the person whose name is on the donation can claim the tax credit.
- Therefore, for a dual-income married couple where both spouses have at least 100,000 KRW in Determined Tax, **each spouse should donate 100,000 KRW under their own name** (totaling 200,000 KRW refunded at 100% + 60,000 KRW in gifts), rather than one person donating 200,000 KRW.

### ③ Expat 19% Flat Tax Filers Caution
Foreign workers in Korea who elect the special **19% Flat Income Tax Rate (`단일세율`)** waive all income deductions and tax credits under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act. Only taxpayers filing under the standard progressive income tax brackets can claim the Hometown Love Donation tax credit.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Do I have to order the return gift before December 31 as well?
**No!** To claim the tax credit for the 2026 tax year, only your **100,000 KRW donation payment** must be completed by December 31. Your 30,000 return gift points remain valid for up to 5 years, so you can pay in December and order your fresh food gift in January or February to avoid year-end shipping congestion.

### Q2. Can I split 100,000 KRW across two different municipalities (50,000 KRW each)?
**Yes**, the tax credit applies to the combined annual total (100,000 KRW = 100% credit). However, you will receive 15,000 points in Municipality A and 15,000 points in Municipality B, which cannot be combined. Since the best gifts cost 30,000 points, donating 100,000 KRW to a single municipality is strongly recommended.

### Q3. Does paying by credit card also count toward my Credit Card Income Deduction?
**No.** Under Korean tax law, donations paid via credit card are excluded from the Credit Card Income Deduction (`신용카드 등 사용금액 소득공제`) because they already receive a much larger direct tax credit (100%). However, your credit card company may still award standard card points depending on your card's terms.

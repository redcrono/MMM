In South Korea's wealth management landscape, the **"Big Three Tax-Advantaged Accounts" (절세 삼총사)—Intermediary ISA (중개형 ISA), Pension Savings Fund (연금저축펀드), and Individual Retirement Pension (개인형 IRP)**—form the foundation of every high-net-worth tax shield. Investing in US index ETFs (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100) or high-dividend ETFs (Korean-listed SCHD) inside a standard brokerage account subjects you to a 15.4% dividend tax on every distribution and a 15.4% capital gains tax on domestic-listed overseas ETFs.

By contrast, allocating your capital across ISA, Pension Savings, and IRP in the right sequence eliminates or defers 100% of that tax drag while returning up to **1,485,000 KRW every year in direct year-end tax refunds**. Here is the definitive side-by-side master comparison.

## 2. Which Assets Belong in Which Account? (Tax-Location Optimization)

Putting the right asset in the wrong account costs millions of won in unnecessary taxes. Follow this institutional asset-location rule:

1. **Intermediary ISA (중개형 ISA):**
   * **Best Assets:** Domestic high-dividend stocks (bank holdings, telecoms), Korean REITs (리츠), Covered Call ETFs, and Domestic-listed Overseas ETFs (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, SCHD) needed within 3–5 years.
   * **Why:** ISA nets losses against gains (손익통산) and shields high cash distributions from the 20M KRW Comprehensive Financial Income Tax cliff.
2. **Pension Savings Fund (연금저축펀드):**
   * **Best Assets:** 100% Aggressive Growth Overseas Equity ETFs (TIGER US Nasdaq 100, KODEX US S&P 500).
   * **Why:** Because capital gains on domestic-listed US ETFs are normally taxed at 15.4%, deferring that tax for 15–25 years inside a 100%-equity Pension Savings Fund maximizes long-term compounding.
3. **Individual IRP (개인형 IRP):**
   * **Best Assets:** 70% US Dividend/Index ETFs + 30% TDF 2050 ETF or US 30-Year Treasury Bond ETF.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. If I have low income or zero income tax payable, should I put money in Pension Savings first or ISA first?**  
A. If your annual determined tax (결정세액) is zero or near zero, you cannot receive a year-end tax credit refund from Pension Savings. In that case, **100% prioritize your Intermediary ISA first** (or deposit into Pension Savings without claiming the tax credit so principal remains freely withdrawable).

**Q2. Does private pension income after age 55 trigger Health Insurance dependent loss?**  
A. No! Private pension payouts (from Pension Savings and IRP) up to **15,000,000 KRW per year** qualify for low-rate separate taxation (3.3%–5.5%) and are **completely excluded** from NHIS dependent income assessments.

**Q3. Can I transfer an existing bank-type ISA or insurance-type Pension Savings to a brokerage firm?**  
A. Yes! Under the Financial Supervisory Service's **Pension & ISA Account Portability System (계좌이동제)**, you can transfer your existing account to any securities brokerage app without losing your original opening date or accumulated tax-free tenure.

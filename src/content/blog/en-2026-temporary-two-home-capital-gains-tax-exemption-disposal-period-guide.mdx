When upgrading from an existing apartment (`종전주택`, Home A) to a new apartment (`신규주택`, Home B) in South Korea, most families briefly own two homes at the same time because closing dates rarely align on the exact same day. Under Korean tax law, missing a single statutory deadline during this transition can disqualify you from the 1-Household 1-Home tax exemption—turning a 0 KRW tax bill into a **100+ million KRW Capital Gains Tax (`양도소득세`) shock**.

Fortunately, **Article 155(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Income Tax Act (`일시적 1세대 2주택 비과세 특례`)** treats qualifying 2-home upgraders identically to 1-homeowners: granting **100% tax exemption up to a 1.2 billion KRW sale price** plus up to an **80% Long-Term Holding Special Deduction** on any excess above 1.2 billion KRW.

In this 2026 guide, we break down **the mandatory 1-2-3 timing rules, how the 1.2 billion KRW proportional formula works, marriage and inheritance 2-home exceptions, and a 4-step transaction checklist**.

## 1. The Mandatory "1-2-3 Rule" for Temporary 2-Home Tax Exemption

In Korean tax law, "acquisition date (`취득일`)" and "transfer date (`양도일`)" are determined by the **final balance payment date (`잔금청산일`) or title registration date (`등기접수일`), whichever is earlier**—NOT the initial contract signing date.

### ① [1-Year Rule] Buy Home B at Least 1 Year After Acquiring Home A
- You must pay the final balance (`잔금`) on your new home (Home B) **at least 1 full year after** the acquisition date of your original home (Home A).
- **Pro Tip:** You *can* sign the purchase contract (`매매계약서`) for Home B before 1 year has passed, as long as the **final balance payment date for Home B is scheduled after the 1-year anniversary** of Home A's acquisition!

### ② [2-Year Rule] Hold (and Reside in) Home A for at Least 2 Years
- Before selling Home A, you must have held Home A for **at least 2 years**.
- If Home A was located in an **Adjustment Target Area (`조정대상지역`) at the time you originally bought it**, you must also complete **2 years of actual physical residence (`실거주 2년`)** in Home A.

### ③ [Disposal Rule] Sell Home A Within the Statutory Disposal Window
- You must complete the sale (receive the final balance) of Home A within **3 years** from the date you acquired Home B (note: if both Home A and Home B are in designated Adjustment Target Areas under the revised enforcement decree, check whether the 2-year window or 3-year transitional clause applies to your contract date).

## 2. How the 1.2 Billion KRW Threshold & 80% Deduction Formula Works

When you qualify for the 1-Household 1-Home (or Temporary 2-Home) exemption and sell Home A for **more than 1.2 billion KRW**, you are **NOT** taxed on the entire capital gain. Instead, only the slice of gain above 1.2 billion KRW is taxable:

> **`[High-Value Home (> 1.2B KRW) Taxable Capital Gain Formula]`**
> * **Step 1 (Total Gain):** `Sale Price - Purchase Price - Necessary Expenses (Acquisition Tax, Brokerage Fee, Capital Improvements)`
> * **Step 2 (Proportional Taxable Gain):** `Total Gain × [ (Sale Price - 1,200,000,000 KRW) ÷ Sale Price ]`
> * **Step 3 (Long-Term Holding Deduction):** `Step 2 Taxable Gain × (1 - Holding/Residence Deduction Rate up to 80%)`
> * **Step 4 (Final Tax Base):** `Step 3 Amount - 2,500,000 KRW Basic Annual Deduction`

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Does joint ownership between spouses (50:50) double the 1.2 billion KRW exemption to 2.4 billion KRW?
**No.** The 1.2 billion KRW tax-free cap applies **per house**, not per person. However, if your apartment sells for more than 1.2 billion KRW, **50:50 spousal joint ownership (`부부 공동명의`) is still much cheaper** than single ownership because the taxable gain above 1.2 billion KRW is split in half—allowing each spouse to claim a 2.5M KRW basic deduction and placing both spouses into much lower progressive income tax brackets (6%～45%).

### Q2. Do pre-sale rights (`분양권`) count as a home for the Temporary 2-Home rules?
**Yes.** All apartment pre-sale rights (`분양권`) acquired on or after January 1, 2021 count toward your household's home count when determining 1-Household 1-Home and Temporary 2-Home exemptions.

### Q3. Which renovation costs can be deducted as "Necessary Expenses (`필요경비`)"?
Only **capital expenditures (`자본적 지출`)** that permanently increase structural value—such as balcony expansion (`발코니 확장`), window sash replacement (`샷시 교체`), system air-conditioner installation, and boiler replacement—are deductible (with official tax invoices, card receipts, or cash receipts). Cosmetic repairs like wallpaper, flooring, and painting are excluded.

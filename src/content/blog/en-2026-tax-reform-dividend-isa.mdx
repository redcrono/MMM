To accelerate capital market revitalization, corporate shareholder return initiatives (dividend hikes and treasury share cancellations), and long-term domestic equity investments, South Korea's Ministry of Economy and Finance (MOEF) unveiled the **2026 Tax Reform Bill**.

For investors previously vulnerable to top marginal income tax brackets of up to 49.5% under the Comprehensive Financial Income Taxation regime (금융소득종합과세 on dividend + interest income exceeding 20M KRW), the reform introduces **Separate Flat Taxation on High-Dividend Corporations** alongside the **Productive Finance ISA (국내투자형 ISA)** offering **100% unlimited tax-free interest and dividends on domestic equities**.

This master guide details the structural provisions of the 2026 Tax Reform, cash-flow simulations on dividend portfolios, and optimal multi-account allocation roadmaps.

## 2. High-Dividend Separation Tax Architecture

Under the revised framework, cash distributions from listed companies meeting statutory shareholder-return criteria (e.g. 3-year consecutive dividend increases or dividend payout ratios ≥ 20%) can be **separated from global composite income**:

| Income Tier | Current Comprehensive Regime (&gt; 20M KRW) | <strong>2026 Revised Separate Tax Option</strong> |
|:---|:---|:---|
| <strong>Base Tier (Up to 20M KRW)</strong> | 14% (15.4% including local income tax) | <strong>14% (15.4% identical)</strong> |
| <strong>Excess Tier (&gt; 20M KRW)</strong> | Aggregated with salary/business income ➔ <strong>14% to 45% (Up to 49.5% max)</strong> | <strong>Elective 25% (27.5% with local tax) Flat Rate</strong> ⭐ |
| <strong>National Health Insurance Impact</strong> | Loss of dependent status if financial income &gt; 20M KRW | <strong>Protective mitigation schedules linked to separate filings</strong> |

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents open the new Productive Finance ISA?
**Yes.** All foreign residents holding an Alien Registration Card (ARC) who qualify as domestic tax residents under the Korean Income Tax Act are fully eligible to open Productive Finance ISAs.

### Q2. Are foreign-listed stocks (e.g. Apple, NVDA) eligible for the Productive Finance ISA?
**No.** The Productive Finance ISA is legally restricted to domestic Korean equities and funds investing in domestic securities. Foreign stocks must be traded in general taxable accounts or via KRX-listed ETFs in Standard ISAs.

### Q3. How do I verify if a Korean stock qualifies as a "High-Dividend Corporation"?
The Korea Exchange (KRX) and Korea Securities Depository publish the official statutory roster of qualified shareholder-return corporations, which is automatically integrated into broker HTS/MTS systems and NTS HomeTax.

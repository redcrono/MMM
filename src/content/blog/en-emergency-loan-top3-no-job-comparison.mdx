This master guide details the top 3 Tier-1 bank products, SGI guarantee mechanics, and alternative credit backup routes.

## 2. Why Zero Income Verification Still Yields Tier-1 Approvals

Instead of examining W-2 tax forms or employment letters, banks rely on **SGI Seoul Guarantee (SGI서울보증)**.
- If you have no outstanding mobile phone bill delinquencies and no severe credit card defaults (≥ 100K KRW for 5+ days), **SGI automatically issues an insurance bond backing 100% of your loan to the bank**.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents holding an ARC apply for 3M KRW emergency loans?
Foreign nationals holding permanent residency (F-5 visa), marriage immigration (F-6 visa), or select resident statuses (F-2) who possess verified domestic credit bureau records can apply for KakaoBank and Toss Bank micro-lines.

### Q2. Can I hold multiple 3M KRW emergency loans at different banks simultaneously?
**No.** All SGI-backed emergency loans share a single global borrower ceiling at SGI Seoul Guarantee (typically 3M–5M KRW total). Opening a second SGI line will be rejected.

### Q3. Are there early repayment penalty fees?
**Zero.** Prepayment fees are 100% waived; depositing funds back into the account terminates interest charges instantaneously.

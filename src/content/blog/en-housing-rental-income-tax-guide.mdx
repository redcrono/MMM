For multi-property owners, landlords, and foreign real estate investors in South Korea receiving monthly rental revenue (월세) or managing Jeonse lump-sum deposits (전세보증금), understanding statutory **Residential Rental Income Tax** is critical during every May Comprehensive Tax filing.

Under the Korean Income Tax Act, landlords with annual gross rental revenue **strictly at or below 20,000,000 KRW** are legally entitled to choose between **14% Flat Separate Taxation** and **Progressive Comprehensive Taxation (6% to 45%)**.

However, choosing the wrong option can trigger severe unintended side effects: **immediate termination of family National Health Insurance (NHIS) Dependent Status, resulting in hundreds of thousands of won in monthly regional health insurance bills**.

This master guide details property-count thresholds, comparative tax simulations, and social insurance protection rules.

## 2. 14% Separate Flat Tax vs. Comprehensive Progressive Tax

For landlords earning **≤ 20 Million KRW in gross annual rent**:

### ① 14% Separate Flat Taxation Formula

> 📌 **[Separate Taxation Calculation]**  
> Taxable Base = Gross Revenue - Statutory Deductible Expenses - Basic Allowance  
> Tax Due = Taxable Base × 14% (15.4% including Local Income Tax)  

- **Registered Rental Housing**: **60% Expense Ratio**, **4,000,000 KRW Allowance** (if other income ≤ 20M KRW).
- **Unregistered Standard Housing**: **50% Expense Ratio**, **2,000,000 KRW Allowance** (if other income ≤ 20M KRW).

### ② Progressive Comprehensive Taxation
Rental revenue is consolidated with employment salary, business income, and dividend income, evaluated under standard progressive rates (6% to 45%).

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. My spouse owns 1 home and I own 1 home. Are we 1-home or 2-home landlords?
**You are 2-home landlords.** Korean tax authorities calculate property counts by combining **both spouses legally**. If either property receives monthly rent, it is subject to taxation in May.

### Q2. Does NTS discover unreported monthly rent if the tenant skips year-end deductions?
**Yes.** South Korea mandates the **Housing Lease Transaction Reporting System (전월세 신고제)**. NTS cross-references court move-in records and registered fixed-date databases to automatically track active leases.

### Q3. Are residential Officetels (오피스텔) counted as residential homes?
If the tenant occupies the Officetel for residential purposes (logging resident registration), the tax code treats it as a residential property, adding to your overall housing count.

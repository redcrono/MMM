Balancing reward points, cash back, and airline miles on credit cards while capturing the maximum **3,000,000 KRW statutory income deduction during the Korean Year-End Tax Settlement (연말정산)** requires mastering the **Credit vs. Debit Card Golden Spending Ratio**.

Using credit cards exclusively often fails to max out the deduction ceiling due to lower deduction rates (15%), whereas using debit cards exclusively forfeits lucrative credit card perks.

This master guide details the 25% gross salary threshold under Article 126-2 of the Restriction of Special Taxation Act, comparative tax refund simulations, excluded spending categories, and dual-income spousal allocation strategies.

## 3. 6 Major Non-Deductible Card Spending Categories

The following expenditures are legally excluded from card tax deductions regardless of payment volume:
1. **New vehicle purchases** (Used cars qualify for a 10% deduction).
2. **National/local taxes, electricity, gas, water bills, and apartment maintenance fees**.
3. **Overseas card transactions and international cross-border e-commerce**.
4. **Gift cards, department store vouchers, and prepaid balances**.
5. **School tuition fees (Elementary, High School, University)**.
6. **Auto lease payments and highway toll passes**.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. If my spouse uses a Family Card under my account, who gets the deduction?
The deduction belongs to the **actual individual card user whose name appears on the plastic card**, not the primary account holder who pays the monthly bill.

### Q2. What is the deduction rate for public transit cards (K-Pass, Climate Card)?
Public transit spending carries an elevated **80% deduction rate**, supplemented by an additional standalone 1,000,000 KRW deduction allowance outside the standard 3M KRW cap.

### Q3. Can foreign residents claim card deductions on HomeTax?
**Yes.** All foreign nationals holding an Alien Registration Card (ARC) who participate in the January Year-End Settlement enjoy full statutory rights to card income deductions.

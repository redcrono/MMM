## 4. Multi-Debt Payoff Prioritization Hierarchy

1. **Highest Interest Rate First**: Private lenders ➔ Card loans / Cash advances ➔ Tier-2 Savings Banks ➔ Tier-1 Commercial Banks.
2. **Consolidate Small Balances**: Having 3+ open loan facilities classifies you as a "Multi-Debt Borrower" (다중채무자). Clear small 1M–3M KRW debts first to reduce total loan facility counts.
3. **Resolve Oldest Delinquencies First**: Delinquency age accelerates scoring deterioration; cure past-due debts in order of age.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Does checking my credit score on Toss or KakaoBank lower my score?
**No.** Under Financial Services Commission (FSC) regulatory standards since 2011, consumer credit inquiries **do not impact credit scores whatsoever**.

### Q2. Does opening an unused Overdraft Line (마이너스통장) hurt my score?
Yes, potentially. Even if drawn balance is 0 KRW, **100% of the approved credit limit counts against your total Debt Service Ratio (DSR) capacity**, dampening KCB debt assessments.

### Q3. Can foreign residents build 900+ credit scores in Korea?
**Yes.** Foreign residents holding an Alien Registration Card (ARC) who establish a 12+ month history of disciplined credit card utilization and utility payments routinely achieve 900+ prime credit scores.

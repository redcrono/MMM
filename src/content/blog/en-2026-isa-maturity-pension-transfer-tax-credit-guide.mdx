When your Korean Individual Savings Account (ISA) reaches its mandatory 3-year holding period, withdrawing funds directly to a standard bank account wastes an extraordinary tax incentive.

Under Article 91-18 of the Restriction of Special Taxation Act, rolling over mature ISA balances into a Pension Savings or IRP account grants an **extra 10% tax credit (capped at ₩3,000,000)** on top of standard retirement deductions.

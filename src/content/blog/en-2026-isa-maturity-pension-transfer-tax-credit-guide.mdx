When your Korean **Individual Savings Account (중개형 ISA)** reaches its mandatory 3-year maturity, simply closing the account and withdrawing the cash to a normal checking account wastes one of the most generous tax loopholes in the Korean tax code. Under **Article 91-18 of the Restriction of Special Taxation Act**, transferring your matured ISA proceeds into a Pension Savings (연금저축) or IRP account **within 60 days of termination unlocks an additional tax credit equal to 10% of the transferred amount, up to 3,000,000 KRW**.

When stacked on top of the standard 9,000,000 KRW annual pension tax credit limit, your total deductible pension contribution jumps to **12,000,000 KRW for that tax year—generating up to 1,980,000 KRW in direct year-end cash refunds**. Here is the exact mathematical and procedural blueprint.

## 2. The Hidden Secret: Why the Remaining 90% (27M KRW) Becomes a Tax-Free Vault

When you roll over 30,000,000 KRW from your matured ISA into a **Pension Savings Fund (연금저축펀드)**:
1. **Only 3,000,000 KRW (10%) receives a tax credit** and becomes subject to future pension income tax (3.3%–5.5% after age 55).
2. **The remaining 27,000,000 KRW (90%) is legally classified as "Non-Deducted Principal (세액공제 받지 않은 원금)."**
   * Inside the Pension Savings account, all dividends and capital gains earned by that 27M KRW enjoy **0% tax withholding (100% tax deferral)** for decades.
   * Even better: whenever you need cash before age 55, you can **withdraw up to the full 27,000,000 KRW non-deducted principal at any time with 0% tax and 0% penalty!**
   * Moreover, ISA maturity rollovers bypass the standard 18,000,000 KRW annual pension contribution cap, allowing you to supercharge your tax-deferred compounding engine.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Should I transfer the ISA maturity funds into Pension Savings (연금저축) or IRP?**  
A. Always choose **Pension Savings Fund (연금저축펀드)**. In an IRP, the 27M KRW non-deducted principal is locked until age 55 (because IRP forbids partial withdrawals), and 30% must be held in safe assets. In a Pension Savings Fund, you can invest 100% in S&P 500 / Nasdaq 100 ETFs and withdraw the 27M KRW principal tax-free whenever needed.

**Q2. Can I transfer only part of my ISA maturity balance (e.g., 30M out of 50M KRW)?**  
A. **Yes!** After terminating your matured ISA, you can keep 20M KRW in cash for personal use and transfer exactly 30M KRW into your Pension Savings account within 60 days to claim the full 3M KRW maximum bonus tax credit.

**Q3. What if I already contributed the maximum 18M KRW to my pension accounts this year?**  
A. Statutory ISA maturity rollovers do NOT count against the 18M KRW annual pension contribution ceiling. You can deposit the entire matured ISA balance on top of your regular 18M KRW contributions.

## 1. The 2026 Marriage Tax Credit (`혼인세액공제`): Up to 1.1 Million KRW Saved

Under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act, any resident taxpayer who officially files their **Marriage Registration (`혼인신고`) between January 1, 2024 and December 31, 2026** qualifies for a direct once-in-a-lifetime tax credit in the tax year of registration:

> **`[Marriage Tax Credit Calculation Formula]`**
> * **National Income Tax Credit:** **500,000 KRW per person** (Husband 500,000 KRW + Wife 500,000 KRW = **1,000,000 KRW** for dual-income couples)
> * **Local Income Tax Automatic Reduction (10%):** **50,000 KRW per person** (**100,000 KRW** combined)
> * **Total Combined Tax Reduction:** Up to **1,100,000 KRW** *(within each spouse's Determined Tax liability)*

## 2. 100% Unlimited Tax Exemption on Corporate Childbirth Subsidies (`기업 출산지원금`)

Under **Article 12(3) of the Income Tax Act**, childbirth bonuses paid by an employer to an employee are **100% tax-exempt with no upper ceiling** (`전액 비과세`), provided three requirements are met:
1. **Paid Within 2 Years of Birth:** The subsidy must be paid within **2 years from the child's date of birth** (up to **2 separate payments** are allowed within that 2-year window).
2. **Official Company Policy:** The bonus must be paid pursuant to universal company compensation rules approved by the board or employment regulations.
3. **Non-Controlling Family Member:** Employees who are relatives (`친족 특수관계인`) of the controlling shareholder or business owner are excluded.

Whether your company pays a **5,000,000 KRW** or **100,000,000 KRW** childbirth bonus, **0 KRW in income tax or NHIS health insurance premium** is deducted!

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can a foreign spouse or an international couple claim the 500,000 KRW Marriage Tax Credit?
**Yes!** If a Korean national and a foreign resident spouse (or two foreign resident taxpayers filing under Korea's standard progressive income tax) officially register their marriage in Korea between 2024 and 2026, each working spouse subject to standard year-end tax settlement can claim the 500,000 KRW Marriage Tax Credit.

### Q2. Does paying for a Postpartum Care Center (`산후조리원`) with the First Meeting Voucher (`첫만남이용권`) qualify for the 2M KRW medical tax credit?
The portion paid using government subsidies (such as the 2,000,000 KRW `첫만남이용권` voucher) is excluded. However, **any out-of-pocket amount you pay above the voucher using your own credit/debit card or cash receipt qualifies up to the full 2,000,000 KRW cap (300,000 KRW tax refund)**.

### Q3. If we register our marriage in December, can we combine medical expenses spent earlier in the same year before we registered?
**Yes.** Spousal status for Year-End Tax Settlement is determined as of **December 31** of the tax year, so medical expenses incurred across the entire calendar year can be combined.

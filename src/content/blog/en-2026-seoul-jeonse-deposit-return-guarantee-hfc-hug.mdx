Jeonse deposit guarantee insurance (전세보증금 반환보증) is South Korea's statutory shield against landlord insolvency. If your landlord fails to return your deposit upon lease expiration, public institutions like **HUG** or **HF** repay the full amount directly to you.

      <li><strong>126% Rule:</strong> Your jeonse deposit plus senior liens must not exceed 126% of the officially published housing price (공시가격).</li>
      <li><strong>No Landlord Consent:</strong> Tenants can unilaterally enroll via mobile apps (Naver Pay, Toss, KakaoPay).</li>
      <li><strong>₩300,000 Fee Refund:</strong> Young adults (under 39) with annual income below ₩50M receive up to ₩300k cash reimbursement from local governments.</li>

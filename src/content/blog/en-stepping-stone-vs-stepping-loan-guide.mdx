When commercial mortgage and Jeonse rental loan interest rates hover at 4.5% to 5.5%, South Korea's state-backed National Housing Urban Fund (NHUF) provides **Didimdol (내집마련 디딤돌) Purchase Mortgages** and **Beotimmok (버팀목) Jeonse Deposit Loans at statutory subsidized rates between 1.8% and 3.95% p.a.**.

For young professionals under age 40, Didimdol offers a unique **Graduated (Stepped) Repayment Schedule (체증식 분할상환)**, minimizing initial monthly debt service to just a few hundred thousand won.

This master guide evaluates Didimdol vs. Beotimmok matrices, 30-year repayment trajectory simulations, and the critical distinction between HUG and HF guarantee mechanisms.

## 2. Graduated (Stepped) Repayment for Borrowers Under Age 40

Borrowers under age 40 qualify for the **Graduated Stepped Repayment Schedule (체증식 분할상환)**:

| Repayment Schedule | Initial Years (1–3) Monthly Payment | Year 15 Monthly Payment | 30-Year Total Interest | Strategic Advantage |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
| **Graduated (Stepped)** | **💰 ~380,000 – 450,000 KRW** | ~850,000 KRW | ~165,000,000 KRW | **Lowest initial cash burden (Top pick for young workers ⭐)** |
| **Equal Amortization (P+I)** | ~780,000 KRW | ~780,000 KRW | ~132,000,000 KRW | Predictable flat monthly budgeting |
| **Equal Principal (P)** | ~1,120,000 KRW (Peak) | ~750,000 KRW | ~105,000,000 KRW | Minimizes lifetime gross interest |

*Graduated repayment cuts initial monthly outflow in half, allowing early-career borrowers to invest surplus cash into higher-yielding assets or execute strategic lump-sum prepayments later.*

## 3. Rate Reductions to Shave an Extra 0.8%p

- **Housing Subscription Savings (청약저축)**: 5+ years (-0.3%p), 10+ years (-0.4%p), **15+ years (-0.5%p)**.
- **Real Estate Electronic Contract**: Executing transactions via MOLIT's certified digital contract portal yields an immediate **-0.1%p discount**.
- **Child Discounts**: 1 child (-0.3%p), 2 children (-0.5%p), 3+ children (-0.7%p).

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can I transition from Beotimmok Jeonse to Didimdol Purchase without penalties?
**Yes.** When transitioning to homeownership, you can execute a Didimdol purchase mortgage, using the disbursement funds to settle the prior Beotimmok Jeonse loan simultaneously on closing day.

### Q2. Is there a Prepayment Penalty on Didimdol loans?
Didimdol assesses a sliding prepayment penalty (maximum 1.2%) for repayments within the **first 3 years**. After 36 months, all prepayments are **100% penalty-free** (Beotimmok Jeonse has 0% prepayment fees at all times).

### Q3. Can foreign residents married to Korean nationals apply?
**Yes.** Multicultural families where one spouse is a Korean citizen and the other is a registered alien (F-6 visa) enjoy identical sovereign rights to Didimdol and Beotimmok subsidized loans.

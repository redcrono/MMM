## 2. Leveraged Traders: Neutralizing Morning Margin Calls

If you maintain active margin debt or receivables, immediate action is required to avoid brokerage-forced liquidations at extreme discounts:

1. **Check Real-Time Collateral Ratio:**
   * Korean brokerage firms mandate a **140% minimum collateral ratio**. If your account falls below this line post-close, you will receive an automated margin deficiency notice.
2. **Transfer Funds Before 08:30 AM KST:**
   * Deposit cash into your uninvested balance or pledge unencumbered shares as eligible collateral before the pre-market auction window.
3. **If You Lack Supplemental Cash:**
   * Broker liquidations execute at **the lower limit (-30%)** during pre-market single-price auctions, inflicting maximum slippage. If capital injection is impossible, proactively trimming positions at market open minimizes punitive execution pricing.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Should I liquidate everything to cash right now?**  
A. Historical data strongly advises against it. When indices drop 7–8% in a single day, peak capitulation is frequently near. Rushing to cash crystallizes paper losses at extreme multi-quarter valuation troughs.

**Q2. How should I monitor overseas markets tonight?**  
A. Watch tonight's US PPI data and upcoming Federal Reserve remarks. Monitor Nasdaq and S&P 500 futures alongside overnight E-mini KOSPI futures through your brokerage platform.

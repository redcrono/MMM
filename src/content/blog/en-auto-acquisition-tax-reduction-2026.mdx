When purchasing a new or used vehicle in South Korea, the most substantial incidental expenditure beyond the vehicle's sticker price is the <strong>Local Vehicle Acquisition Tax (자동차 취등록세), levied at 7% of the vehicle acquisition cost for standard passenger cars</strong>.

For instance, purchasing a 50 Million KRW family SUV or Electric Vehicle incurs 3.5 Million KRW in local tax.

However, under South Korea's <strong>Local Tax Exemption Control Act</strong>, qualifying for <strong>Eco-Friendly Vehicle Credits (EV/FCEV), Multi-Child Household Exemptions, or Compact Car Allowances can legally reduce your tax bill to zero or save millions of won</strong>.

This master guide breaks down statutory tax schedules, used car valuation rules, and the mandatory 1-year holding requirement to prevent clawback penalties.

## 2. Top 4 Vehicle Acquisition Tax Discounts

### ① Eco-Friendly Vehicles (EV / Hydrogen / Hybrid)
- <strong>Electric Vehicles (EV)</strong>: Direct deduction of <strong>up to 1,400,000 KRW</strong> (If tax is ≤ 1.4M, tax due is 0 KRW).
- <strong>Hydrogen Vehicles (FCEV)</strong>: Direct deduction of <strong>up to 1,400,000 KRW</strong>.
- <strong>Hybrid (HEV)</strong>: Subject to local council sunset provisions (up to 400,000 KRW allowance).


> 📌 <strong>[50M KRW Electric Vehicle Acquisition Tax Calculation]</strong>  
> • Baseline Tax: 50,000,000 KRW × 7% = 3,500,000 KRW  
> • EV Tax Exemption: -1,400,000 KRW  
> • Final Tax Billed = 2,100,000 KRW (Save 1.4M KRW Cash!)  


### ② Multi-Child Household Exemptions (Children Under Age 18)
- <strong>3 or More Children</strong>:
  - <strong>7 to 10-Passenger Vehicles (Carnival, Palisade)</strong> or Vans ≤ 15 seats: <strong>100% Full Tax Exemption (0 KRW Tax!)</strong>
  - <strong>Standard Sedans (≤ 6 seats)</strong>: Tax exempt up to 1.4M KRW; <strong>85% discount on excess amount</strong>.
- <strong>2-Child Families</strong>: Municipalities grant an expanded <strong>50% local tax reduction</strong> under low-birthrate demographic relief initiatives.

### ③ Compact Car 500,000 KRW Deduction (Ray, Morning, Casper)
- 4% rate applied, then 500,000 KRW subtracted.
- <strong>Vehicles under ~12.5M KRW</strong>: `12.5M × 4% = 500,000 KRW ➔ 0 KRW Tax Due`.
- <strong>Kia Ray at 18M KRW</strong>: `18M × 4% = 720,000 KRW - 500,000 KRW = 220,000 KRW Final Tax`.

## 3. Used Car Valuation & Bond Discount Strategies

When buying used vehicles, the tax base is the <strong>higher of the actual contract purchase price or the official government standard valuation (시가표준액)</strong>.
- Always cross-verify on WeTax that the dealer's tax invoice reflects the pure vehicle sales price, excluding dealer processing commissions.
- <strong>Municipal Subway/Regional Development Bonds</strong>: Instead of purchasing millions in government bonds, select <strong>Immediate Discount Bond Sale (공채 할인 매도)</strong> on registration day to settle the bond requirement for a negligible fraction of the cost.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can multi-child families claim exemptions on 2 vehicles simultaneously?
<strong>No.</strong> Multi-child tax exemptions are strictly limited to <strong>1 vehicle per household at any given time</strong>. You can only claim a new exemption if the prior vehicle is sold or scrapped.

### Q2. Can expat residents stack national EV subsidies with the 1.4M KRW tax discount?
<strong>Yes, 100% stackable.</strong> Ministry of Environment national EV cash subsidies reduce the vehicle purchase price directly, while the 1.4M KRW WeTax credit eliminates local acquisition taxes independently.

### Q3. Is joint marital registration eligible for multi-child tax relief?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Registering the vehicle under single ownership or <strong>joint spousal ownership qualifies for 100% of statutory multi-child tax exemptions</strong>.

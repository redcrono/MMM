When purchasing a new or used vehicle in South Korea, the most substantial incidental expenditure beyond the vehicle's sticker price is the **Local Vehicle Acquisition Tax (자동차 취등록세), levied at 7% of the vehicle acquisition cost for standard passenger cars**.

For instance, purchasing a 50 Million KRW family SUV or Electric Vehicle incurs 3.5 Million KRW in local tax.

However, under South Korea's **Local Tax Exemption Control Act**, qualifying for **Eco-Friendly Vehicle Credits (EV/FCEV), Multi-Child Household Exemptions, or Compact Car Allowances can legally reduce your tax bill to zero or save millions of won**.

This master guide breaks down statutory tax schedules, used car valuation rules, and the mandatory 1-year holding requirement to prevent clawback penalties.

## 2. Top 4 Vehicle Acquisition Tax Discounts

### ① Eco-Friendly Vehicles (EV / Hydrogen / Hybrid)
- **Electric Vehicles (EV)**: Direct deduction of **up to 1,400,000 KRW** (If tax is ≤ 1.4M, tax due is 0 KRW).
- **Hydrogen Vehicles (FCEV)**: Direct deduction of **up to 1,400,000 KRW**.
- **Hybrid (HEV)**: Subject to local council sunset provisions (up to 400,000 KRW allowance).

```
[50M KRW Electric Vehicle Acquisition Tax Calculation]
• Baseline Tax: 50,000,000 KRW × 7% = 3,500,000 KRW
• EV Tax Exemption: -1,400,000 KRW
• Final Tax Billed = 2,100,000 KRW (Save 1.4M KRW Cash!)
```

### ② Multi-Child Household Exemptions (Children Under Age 18)
- **3 or More Children**:
  - **7 to 10-Passenger Vehicles (Carnival, Palisade)** or Vans ≤ 15 seats: **100% Full Tax Exemption (0 KRW Tax!)**
  - **Standard Sedans (≤ 6 seats)**: Tax exempt up to 1.4M KRW; **85% discount on excess amount**.
- **2-Child Families**: Municipalities grant an expanded **50% local tax reduction** under low-birthrate demographic relief initiatives.

### ③ Compact Car 500,000 KRW Deduction (Ray, Morning, Casper)
- 4% rate applied, then 500,000 KRW subtracted.
- **Vehicles under ~12.5M KRW**: `12.5M × 4% = 500,000 KRW ➔ 0 KRW Tax Due`.
- **Kia Ray at 18M KRW**: `18M × 4% = 720,000 KRW - 500,000 KRW = 220,000 KRW Final Tax`.

## 3. Used Car Valuation & Bond Discount Strategies

When buying used vehicles, the tax base is the **higher of the actual contract purchase price or the official government standard valuation (시가표준액)**.
- Always cross-verify on WeTax that the dealer's tax invoice reflects the pure vehicle sales price, excluding dealer processing commissions.
- **Municipal Subway/Regional Development Bonds**: Instead of purchasing millions in government bonds, select **Immediate Discount Bond Sale (공채 할인 매도)** on registration day to settle the bond requirement for a negligible fraction of the cost.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can multi-child families claim exemptions on 2 vehicles simultaneously?
**No.** Multi-child tax exemptions are strictly limited to **1 vehicle per household at any given time**. You can only claim a new exemption if the prior vehicle is sold or scrapped.

### Q2. Can expat residents stack national EV subsidies with the 1.4M KRW tax discount?
**Yes, 100% stackable.** Ministry of Environment national EV cash subsidies reduce the vehicle purchase price directly, while the 1.4M KRW WeTax credit eliminates local acquisition taxes independently.

### Q3. Is joint marital registration eligible for multi-child tax relief?
**Yes.** Registering the vehicle under single ownership or **joint spousal ownership qualifies for 100% of statutory multi-child tax exemptions**.

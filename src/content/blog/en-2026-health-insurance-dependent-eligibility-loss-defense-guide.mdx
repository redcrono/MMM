Here is the comprehensive breakdown of the 2026 NHIS dependent eligibility criteria, the spousal joint-disqualification mechanism, and **4 legitimate asset structuring shields** to protect your household before the November reassessment.

> In NHIS statutory law, married couples are treated as an inseparable financial unit. If a retired husband collects ₩21 million in public pension and loses his dependent status, his wife (who has zero personal income) is **automatically stripped of her dependent status at the exact same moment**.

## 2. The ₩20M Financial Income Cliff: A Real-Life Simulation

In standard progressive taxation, earning an extra ₩100,000 only increases taxes on that marginal ₩100,000. However, in the Korean National Health Insurance system, financial income operates under an **All-or-Nothing binary rule**:

* **Financial Income of ₩19,990,000:** Evaluated as **₩0** for NHIS purposes. Zero premiums owed; dependent status preserved.
* **Financial Income of ₩20,010,000:** The entire ₩20,010,000 is reported. Dependent status is canceled; premiums are assessed on total income plus home equity.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Do capital gains from US stocks (해외주식 양도소득세) trigger NHIS dependent disqualification?
**No.** Under current National Health Insurance Act provisions, capital gains (양도소득) and severance pay (퇴직소득) are categorized as temporary, non-recurring income streams and are **completely excluded from health insurance premium assessments**. Even if you realize ₩50 million in capital gains from US equities, it will not count toward the ₩20M ceiling. (However, any **cash dividends** received from US stocks do count toward the ₩20M financial income limit.)

### Q2. I earned ₩100,000 from occasional 3.3% freelance work. Will I lose dependent status?
* **If you have a formal Business Registration (사업자등록):** Yes, generating even ₩1 of net profit will result in disqualification.
* **If you are an unregistered freelancer (3.3% withholding):** You retain dependent status as long as your net annual freelance income remains **under ₩5,000,000**.

### Q3. If I am disqualified, can I re-qualify as a dependent later?
**Yes, immediately.** If you reduce your dividend income below ₩20M in the subsequent year or close a loss-making registered business, you can submit an application for Dependent Qualification along with proof of reduced income to your local NHIS office to reinstate dependent coverage under your family member.

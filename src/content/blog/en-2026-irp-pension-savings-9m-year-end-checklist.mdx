When preparing for South Korea's annual Year-End Tax Settlement (연말정산), the single most lucrative and guaranteed cash refund mechanism available to salaried workers is maximizing the **9,000,000 KRW combined annual tax credit limit across Pension Savings (연금저축) and Individual Retirement Pension (개인형 IRP) accounts**.

Unlike an income deduction (소득공제), which merely reduces your taxable base, a **tax credit (세액공제)** directly subtracts cash from your final tax bill. Contributing the full 9 million KRW guarantees a direct cash refund of **up to 1,485,000 KRW (16.5%)**—an instant, risk-free return before you even invest a single won inside the account. Here is the complete 4th-quarter execution playbook.

## 2. Why Split 6M into Pension Savings + 3M into IRP? (Portfolio Architecture)

Although an IRP allows you to deposit the full 9 million KRW into a single account, seasoned financial planners almost always recommend **putting 6 million KRW into a Brokerage Pension Savings Fund (연금저축펀드) first, and only the remaining 3 million KRW into an IRP**:

1. **100% Equity Allocation Freedom vs. The 70% Rule:**
   * In a **Pension Savings Fund**, you can invest 100% of your capital in high-growth equity ETFs (e.g., TIGER US S&P 500, KODEX US Nasdaq 100, ACE US Dividend Dow Jones).
   * In an **IRP**, Korean retirement pension law restricts "risky assets" (equities) to a **maximum of 70%**, forcing at least 30% into safe assets.
   * **Pro Tip to Achieve ~94% Equity in IRP:** Instead of leaving the mandatory 30% IRP safe-asset bucket in low-yield cash deposits, invest that 30% into a **Target Date Fund (TDF 2050/2060 ETF)** or a **US Tech + Bond Balanced ETF (채권혼합형, 40% Equity / 60% US Treasury)**—both of which are legally classified as 100% safe assets!
2. **Partial Withdrawal Flexibility:**
   * Pension Savings allows partial withdrawals at any time (and principal that did not receive a tax credit can be withdrawn 100% tax-free). IRP strictly prohibits partial withdrawals except for statutory emergencies (bankruptcy, 6+ months medical care, first-time housing purchase).

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Do I have to buy ETFs before December 31 to get the tax credit, or is depositing cash enough?**  
A. **Depositing cash into the pension account is 100% sufficient!** As long as the KRW cash arrives in your Pension Savings or IRP account before the year-end settlement cutoff on December 31, the full tax credit is recognized even if you buy ETFs gradually in January or February.

**Q2. What happens if I close my pension account before age 55?**  
A. If you withdraw funds that received a tax credit (or investment gains) outside of statutory pension payments after age 55, a **16.5% Other Income Tax (기타소득세)** is withheld. Therefore, only contribute long-term capital or use excess non-deducted contributions for emergency liquidity.

**Q3. What if I deposit 12 million KRW this year (exceeding the 9M limit)?**  
A. You can contribute up to 18 million KRW per year across all pension accounts. For the extra 3 million KRW that did not receive a tax credit this year, you can either **(a) withdraw it tax-free anytime**, or **(b) apply for 'Carryover Tax Credit Conversion (이연 신청)'** next year so it counts toward next year's 9 million KRW tax credit!

## 1. The Triple-Crown Architecture: Savings + Subscription + 2.2% Mortgage

Operated by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT) and the National Housing and Urban Fund (주택도시기금), the **Youth Housing Dream Subscription Account (청년주택드림청약통장)** solves the biggest dilemma facing young adults in South Korea: keeping money locked in a low-interest subscription account just to maintain apartment lottery points, only to fail to secure a mortgage after winning a presale due to strict DSR limits.

This upgraded account connects **asset accumulation (4.5% yield + tax deductions)** directly to **home purchase financing (up to 80% LTV at 2.2%–3.9% over 40 years)**. Furthermore, unlike standard subscription accounts where any withdrawal forces complete account termination, Youth Housing Dream subscribers who win an apartment lottery are permitted a **one-time partial withdrawal** of their accumulated balance to pay the initial presale contract down payment (계약금) while keeping the account alive!

## 3. Deep Dive: The Linked 2.2% Youth Housing Dream Mortgage

The true financial prize of this account is unlocked when you win an apartment presale lottery (청약 당첨). Provided you have held the Youth Housing Dream Account for **at least 1 year** and accumulated **at least ₩10,000,000 in deposits**, you become eligible for the **Youth Housing Dream Loan (청년주택드림대출)** when paying your apartment balance (잔금):
- **Eligible Property:** Presale price **₩600,000,000 or less** and exclusive area **85㎡ or less** (100㎡ in non-urban townships).
- **Borrower Income at Loan Application:** Single ≤ **₩70,000,000** / Newlywed or Married Couple Combined ≤ **₩100,000,000**.
- **LTV & Term:** Up to **80% of the presale price** for up to **40 years**.
- **Life-Cycle Rate Cuts:** Starting from a base floor of **2.2%**, borrowers receive automatic interest rate reductions for major life milestones after loan origination: **-0.1%p for marriage**, **-0.5%p for first childbirth**, and **-0.2%p per additional child** (down to a statutory floor of **1.5% p.a.**).

## 4. How to Convert Your Existing Account & Maximize Recognized Monthly Deposits

Beginning in late 2024 and continuing through 2026, the National Housing and Urban Fund raised the **maximum recognized monthly deposit for public housing (공공분양 월 납입 인정액)** from ₩100,000 to **₩250,000 per month**. Therefore, if you are targeting public presale apartments (such as LH New:Home or SH presales where winners are chosen by total recognized savings balance), increasing your monthly automatic transfer to **₩250,000** maximizes both your public lottery ranking and your ₩3,000,000 annual income tax deduction (`₩250,000 × 12 = ₩3,000,000`).

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. If I live with my parents who own an apartment, am I still considered "homeless" for enrollment?
**Yes!** Unlike the standard subscription income tax deduction (which requires the entire household to be homeless), **enrollment** in the Youth Housing Dream Account only checks whether **you personally (본인 기준 무주택)** own a home. Even if your parents own an apartment in Seoul and you are registered on their resident registration card, you can open or convert to the Youth Housing Dream Account and earn 4.5% interest.

### Q2. When I convert my old account, does the 4.5% interest rate apply to my existing balance?
When you convert a standard subscription account, your existing principal balance is transferred as the initial lump sum of the new account. However, to prevent retroactive windfalls, accrued interest up to the conversion date is paid out at your old account's rate, and the **up to 4.5% preferential interest rate** applies strictly to **new deposits made after the conversion date** (while the transferred principal continues to earn the standard subscription rate of up to 3.1%).

### Q3. What happens after I turn 35 or my salary exceeds ₩50M?
Eligibility is checked **only once at the moment of account opening or conversion**. Even if you get promoted next year and earn ₩85M, or celebrate your 36th birthday, your Youth Housing Dream Account and its 4.5% interest rate remain valid until maturity or apartment winning.

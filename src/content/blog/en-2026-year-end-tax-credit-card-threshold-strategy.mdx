In mid-October, South Korea's National Tax Service (NTS) officially opens the **Year-End Tax Settlement Preview (연말정산 미리보기)** service on Hometax.

The Korean card expense deduction operates under a unique two-stage rule: **Zero deduction until total card expenditure surpasses 25% of your gross annual salary**, followed by differential deduction rates (15% for credit cards vs 30% for debit/check cards and cash receipts).

Here is how wage earners and expats in Korea can optimize their payment mix in Q4 to maximize February cash tax refunds.

## 1. Deduction Rates by Payment Instrument

Under Article 126-2 of the Restriction of Special Taxation Act:

| Payment Method | Deduction Rate | Statutory Limit |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Credit Card** | **15%** | Basic ₩3.0M cap (salary ≤ ₩70M) |
| **Check / Debit Card** | **30%** | Twice the efficiency of credit cards |
| **Traditional Markets** | **40% - 80%** | Additional ₩1M-₩2M separate allowance |

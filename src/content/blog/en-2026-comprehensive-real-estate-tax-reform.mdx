For residential property owners and foreign investors in South Korea, the **Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax (종합부동산세 or 'Jongbuse')** represents the most consequential annual property holding levy.

Under the **2026 Property Tax Reform**, the baseline statutory tax exemption for actual-resident single homeowners has been **expanded from 1.2 Billion to 1.4 Billion KRW in officially assessed value (equivalent to ~2.0 Billion KRW / $1.5M in market price)**, completely exempting the vast majority of middle-class primary residences.

Furthermore, punitive multi-home progressive surcharges based purely on property count have been **repealed in favor of a normalized single rate schedule based strictly on total aggregate assessed value**.

This master guide details the 2026 statutory framework, property value holding tax simulations, and joint-ownership tax optimization strategies.

## 2. Statutory Assessment Formula


> 📌 **[Statutory Holding Tax Assessment Formula]**  
> Tax Base = (Total National Assessed Price - Base Exemption) × Fair Market Value Ratio (60%)  
> * Base Exemption: 1.4 Billion KRW (1-Homeowner) / 900 Million KRW (Standard / Multi-Home)  
> * Tax Assessed = Tax Base × Statutory Tax Rate (0.5% – 2.7%) - Duplicate Local Property Tax Credit - Elderly/Long-term Deductions (Up to 80%)

## 4. Spousal Joint Ownership vs. Single Ownership

- **Joint Ownership (50:50)**: Each spouse receives a separate 900 Million KRW personal exemption, providing a **combined 1.8 Billion KRW total deduction (equivalent to ~2.6 Billion KRW market value) at 0 KRW tax**.
- **Single Ownership Election**: If the property owner is aged 60+ or has held the home for &gt; 5 years, electing single-ownership special rules in September allows applying the **1.4B KRW base deduction plus up to 80% elderly/long-term tax credits**.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents claim the 1.4 Billion KRW single homeowner deduction?
**Yes.** Foreign nationals holding an Alien Registration Card (ARC) who qualify as domestic tax residents and own a single residential home in South Korea for primary residence are **fully entitled to the 1.4 Billion KRW base deduction**.

### Q2. Are inherited properties counted toward total property counts?
Inherited residential properties are **excluded from property count determinations for up to 5 years**, preserving your single-homeowner 1.4B KRW base deduction and senior tax credits.

### Q3. When is the Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax billed and due?
Tax notices are dispatched by the National Tax Service in late November, with payment due annually between **December 1st and December 15th**.

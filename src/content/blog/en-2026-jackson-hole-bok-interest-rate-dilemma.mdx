## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. How should investors position US portfolios post-Jackson Hole?
Given potential volatility in tech multiples, maintain a **20%–30% cash buffer in short-term treasury vehicles (SGOV) or high-yield digital accounts** to capitalize on market corrections.

### Q2. Is it advantageous to refinance variable mortgages into fixed loans now?
**Yes.** Korean commercial banks offer 5-year fixed loans at rates approximately **0.6%–0.9%p lower than variable mortgages**. If your loan has exceeded 3 years, prepayment fees are 100% waived.

### Q3. How does KRW fluctuation impact foreign residents earning in Korea?
When the KRW weakens against the USD, foreign remittances become more costly. Transferring funds during localized dips (USD/KRW ~1,320–1,330) maximizes home currency conversion value.

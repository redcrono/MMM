Given the persistent risks of "Can-Jeonse (깡통전세)" and landlord defaults across South Korea's villa and residential studio rental markets, enrolling in a **Jeonse Deposit Return Guarantee Insurance Policy (HUG, HF, or SGI)** is the indispensable legal safeguard protecting a tenant's life savings.

To encourage full insurance adoption among young adults and low-to-middle income renters, the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT) and municipal governments operate the **Jeonse Deposit Return Guarantee Insurance Premium Subsidy Program**, reimbursing **up to 300,000 KRW in 100% direct cash refunds for paid insurance fees**.

This master guide details income criteria, exclusions, and the streamlined Gov.kr digital submission protocol.

## 2. 3 Key Statutory Exclusion Rules

Applicants are ineligible if:
1. **Tenants in Registered Rental Housing (등록임대주택)**: Commercial corporate landlords are legally mandated to pay 75% of guarantee fees under the Special Act on Private Rental Housing.
2. **Corporate Entity Leases**: Only natural individual non-homeowners qualify.
3. **Public Housing Residents**: LH, SH, or GH public housing occupants face zero landlord default risk and are excluded.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents holding an ARC apply for this subsidy?
**Yes.** Foreign nationals registered as permanent residents (F-5 visa) or married to Korean citizens (F-6 visa) who reside in eligible housing and hold a valid HUG/HF/SGI policy can claim the 300,000 KRW cash rebate.

### Q2. Can I re-apply when renewing my Jeonse lease contract after 2 years?
**Yes.** If you renew your lease and pay a new guarantee premium for an extended 2-year term, you are **legally entitled to re-apply and claim an additional 300,000 KRW subsidy reimbursement**.

### Q3. Can I apply if I purchased the policy several months ago?
**Yes.** As long as your guarantee policy is **active and unexpired on the date of your application**, you are fully eligible for retroactive fee reimbursement regardless of purchase date.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Does clicking Toss Bank's 'Receive Interest Now' increase real compounding returns?
**Yes.** Claiming daily interest adds accrued earnings to your baseline principal immediately, activating **Daily Compounding (일복리)** for all subsequent interest calculations.

### Q2. Can expat residents open high-yield savings bank parking accounts?
**Yes.** Foreign residents registered with an Alien Registration Card (ARC) can open non-face-to-face accounts on mobile apps (KakaoBank, Toss, SB TalkTalk Plus) upon completing digital identity verification.

### Q3. Is un-insured Commercial Paper (발행어음) truly safe?
Under Korean financial regulations, Commercial Paper issuance licenses are strictly limited to **Mega Investment Banks with capital reserves exceeding 4 Trillion KRW**. Zero defaults have occurred since inception, making them practically equivalent to sovereign instruments.

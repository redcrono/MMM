When concluding an employment contract, changing jobs, or retiring in South Korea, statutory **severance pay (퇴직급여 / Toejikgeum)** represents a cornerstone of accumulated personal wealth.

While Korean retirement income is classified separately (**분류과세**) and does not aggregate into annual global income tax brackets, senior employees and long-tenured professionals can still face retirement income taxes running into tens of millions of won if cashed out directly into a standard checking account.

By transferring severance lump-sums directly into an **Individual Retirement Pension (IRP) account and withdrawing funds as an annuity after age 55, Korean tax law legally reduces your retirement income tax liability by 30% to 40% permanently**.

This master guide breaks down statutory calculation formulas, years-of-service deductions, tax deferral compounding benefits, and annual pension withdrawal caps.

## 1. Retirement Income Tax Calculation & Years-of-Service Deductions

To prevent penalizing multi-decade lump sums with high marginal rates, the Korean tax code applies **Years-of-Service Deductions (근속연수공제)** and an **Annualized Multiplier (연분연승법)**:

<InfographicCard title="4-Stage Retirement Income Tax Calculation Framework" type="primary" subtitle="Income Tax Act Articles 48 & 55">
  <div style="background: var(--bg-muted); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: var(--radius-sm); font-family: monospace; font-size: 0.9rem; line-height: 1.8; color: var(--text-main);">
    <strong>Stage 1 [Service Deduction]</strong> = Gross Severance - Service Duration Deduction<br />
    <strong>Stage 2 [Annualized Wage]</strong> = (Post-Deduction Balance ÷ Service Years) × 12<br />
    <strong>Stage 3 [Taxable Base]</strong> = Annualized Wage - Standard Progressive Wage Deductions<br />
    <strong>Stage 4 [Final Tax Due]</strong> = (Taxable Base × 6%–45% Progressive Rate - Allowance) ÷ 12 × Service Years
  </div>
</InfographicCard>

## 3. Statutory Annual Pension Withdrawal Limit Formula

To preserve the 30%–40% tax discount, withdrawals must remain strictly within statutory annual limits:


> 📌 **[Annual Pension Withdrawal Limit Formula]**  
> Maximum Annual Withdrawal = (Account Value at Year Start ÷ (11 - Withdrawal Year)) × 120%  


- **Example**: For a 100M KRW IRP balance in Year 1:
  `(100M KRW ÷ 10) × 120% = Up to 12,000,000 KRW allowed tax-free per year`.
- Withdrawals exceeding this annual ceiling are subject to the **original un-discounted 100% tax rate on the excess portion**.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. What happens if I close my IRP before age 55 due to an emergency?
**No harsh penalties apply to severance capital.** If you liquidate an IRP containing severance rollover funds before age 55, you are **not charged the 16.5% other income tax penalty on severance principal—you simply pay the deferred original retirement tax**. (Only voluntary personal contributions claiming annual tax deductions trigger the 16.5% penalty.)

### Q2. I already received severance into my salary checking account with tax withheld. Can I undo this?
**Yes, within a 60-day window!** If you deposit the severance funds into an IRP within **60 calendar days from the receipt date**, the financial institution will process an official tax refund, restoring your full pre-tax capital.

### Q3. Can foreign residents and expats open and hold an IRP account?
**Yes.** Any foreign national registered with an Alien Registration Card (ARC) participating in Korea's 4 major social insurances is eligible to maintain an IRP account and utilize identical 30%–40% tax discount schedules.

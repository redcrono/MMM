Rolling your severance pay (퇴직금) directly into an IRP account defers 100% of your severance tax and reduces your ultimate retirement tax bill by 30% to 40%.

Navigating personal finance, taxation, and government programs in South Korea can feel complex, but understanding the foundational rules allows you to maximize statutory benefits and protect your hard-earned wealth.

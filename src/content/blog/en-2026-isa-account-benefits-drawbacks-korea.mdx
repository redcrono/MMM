When building a high-yield dividend or index fund portfolio in South Korea, standard taxable accounts relentlessly shave <strong>15.4% in dividend and interest income taxes</strong> from every payout.

The most potent tax shield available in the Korean market is the <strong>Brokerage ISA (중개형 개인종합자산관리계좌 / Individual Savings Account)</strong>.

Under South Korea's <strong>2026 tax reforms, the statutory tax-free net profit threshold has expanded up to 10 Million KRW</strong>, with annual contribution ceilings doubled to 40 Million KRW (200M KRW lifetime total). 

This comprehensive master guide details eligibility tiers (General vs. Working-Class), profit-loss aggregation mechanics (손익통산), and practical strategies to bypass the 3-year holding constraint.

## 3. The 3 Constraints of the ISA & How to Overcome Them

### ⚠️ Constraint 1: Direct US Stock Trading Prohibited
- <strong>Limitation</strong>: You cannot purchase direct US tickers like NVDA, AAPL, or VOO directly inside an ISA.
- <strong>Solution</strong>: Purchase <strong>domestic Korean-listed US ETFs</strong> such as <strong>`TIGER S&P500`</strong>, <strong>`ACE Nasdaq100`</strong>, or <strong>`SOL US Dividend Dow Jones (SCHD)`</strong>. These track identical US indices while unlocking full ISA tax exemption on distributions.

### ⚠️ Constraint 2: Mandatory 3-Year Holding Period
- <strong>Common Myth</strong>: Investors assume all capital is locked for 3 years.
- <strong>Reality</strong>: <strong>Contributed principal can be withdrawn at any time without fees or tax penalties</strong>. Only accumulated capital gains are finalized upon official 3-year maturity.

### ⚠️ Constraint 3: One Account Per Individual
- <strong>Strategy</strong>: Upon reaching 3-year maturity, terminate the account to capture your tax-free gains, roll over capital into an IRP to claim up to 3M KRW in additional tax credits, and <strong>immediately open a new 3-year ISA to reset the 5M–10M KRW exemption quota</strong>.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. If I withdraw principal during the 3 years, does my annual deposit quota restore?
<strong>No.</strong> If you deposit 20M KRW and withdraw 10M KRW in the same tax year, your cumulative annual deposit limit remains consumed at 20M KRW. However, unused deposit allowances roll over into subsequent calendar years.

### Q2. Can foreign nationals and expats open a Brokerage ISA?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Foreign residents holding a registered Alien Registration Card (ARC) who are tax residents in South Korea and have not been subject to Financial Income Comprehensive Taxation in the preceding 3 years are fully eligible to open a Brokerage ISA.

### Q3. What happens if my holdings are down at the 3-year maturity mark?
You are not required to liquidate at a loss. Simply submit a <strong>Maturity Extension Request</strong> through your brokerage app to extend your account holding duration while retaining full tax-free status until markets recover.

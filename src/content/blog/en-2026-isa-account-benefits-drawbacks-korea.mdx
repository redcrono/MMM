When investing in dividend stocks or US index ETFs in South Korea, paying 15.4% dividend income tax on all earnings quickly eats into your compound growth.

The **Individual Savings Account (ISA - 개인종합자산관리계좌)** solves this by offering **up to 5,000,000 KRW (10,000,000 KRW for standard income earners) in 100% tax-free capital gains and dividends**, with excess gains taxed at a low 9.9% separate rate.

## 2. The Power of Loss Offsetting (손익통산)

In a regular brokerage account, profits on Stock A (10M KRW) are taxed at 15.4% (1.54M KRW tax) even if you lost 4M KRW on Stock B.

In an **ISA Account**, your net profit is calculated as 10M - 4M = **6,000,000 KRW**. 
- 5,000,000 KRW is **100% Tax-Free (0 KRW tax)**
- The remaining 1,000,000 KRW is taxed at only 9.9% (**99,000 KRW tax**)
- **Total Savings: 1,441,000 KRW in tax savings!**

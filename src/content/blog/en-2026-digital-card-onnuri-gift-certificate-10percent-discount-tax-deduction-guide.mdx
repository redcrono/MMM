## 1. The Triple-Stack Arbitrage of Korea's Digital Card-Type Onnuri System

Issued by the Ministry of SMEs and Startups and the Small Enterprise and Market Service (SEMAS), **Onnuri Gift Certificates (`온누리상품권`)** were historically sold as paper vouchers (`지류 상품권`) used at traditional wet markets. However, the launch of the **Digital Card-Type Onnuri Gift Certificate (`충전식 카드형 온누리상품권`)** transformed the program into South Korea's most powerful everyday household savings tool.

Instead of carrying paper vouchers or scanning QR codes, you simply register the Korean credit or check card already in your wallet inside the official `온누리상품권` mobile app. Every time you swipe your linked card at a registered merchant, three separate financial benefits stack simultaneously:

> **`Total Effective Savings Rate = 10% Instant Top-Up Discount + 1%～3% Credit Card Cashback/Points (Counts toward Monthly Card Spend!) + 40% Year-End Tax Deduction (～6.6% to 10.5% Tax Refund) = 17.6% to 23.5% Total Return!`**

## 3. Where to Spend It: Alley Shopping Districts (`골목형상점가`) & The Split-Payment Trap

### ① Hidden Urban Merchants: Clinics, Optical Shops, Bakeries & BBQ Restaurants
Thanks to local governments designating hundreds of urban commercial streets as **Alley Shopping Districts (`골목형상점가`)**, you do not need to visit a traditional outdoor market to use your Onnuri balance. Inside the `온누리상품권` app's **[가맹점 찾기 (Find Merchants)]** map, you will find neighborhood **Korean BBQ restaurants, butcher shops, fruit grocers, pharmacies, dental clinics, Korean medicine clinics (`한의원`), optical stores (glasses/contact lenses), and private academies** that accept Card-Type Onnuri payments.

### ② The #1 Trap to Avoid: No Automatic Split Payment!
If your Onnuri app balance is **KRW 25,000** and your restaurant bill is **KRW 30,000**, swiping your linked credit card will **NOT** deduct KRW 25,000 from your Onnuri balance and charge KRW 5,000 to your card. Instead, because the Onnuri balance is insufficient to cover the full KRW 30,000 bill, the system **bypasses your Onnuri balance completely and charges the entire KRW 30,000 as a regular un-discounted credit card transaction**! Always check your app balance before swiping—or ask the cashier to split the bill into two swipes.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Does tapping with Apple Pay or KakaoPay/Naver Pay QR work with the Card-Type Onnuri balance?
**A.** **Samsung Pay works 100% seamlessly** with your linked card, because MST/NFC transmits your registered card credentials to the merchant terminal. However, **Apple Pay** and QR/barcode wallets (`카카오페이`, `네이버페이`) often generate tokenized virtual card numbers that fail to match the card number registered in the Onnuri database—causing the charge to go through as a regular full-price card payment. iPhone users should always swipe or insert the **physical plastic card** at Onnuri merchants.

### Q2. How much extra tax refund does the 40% Traditional Market Deduction give me during Year-End Tax Settlement?
**A.** Once your total annual card spend exceeds 25% of your gross salary, every Won spent via Card-Type Onnuri is deducted from your taxable income at **40%**—more than double the standard credit card deduction rate (15%)! Furthermore, even if you max out your basic KRW 3,000,000 card deduction cap, Traditional Market spending unlocks a **separate bonus deduction ceiling of KRW 1,000,000** (up to KRW 3,000,000 combined with public transit and cultural expenses).

### Q3. Can I buy groceries online using my Card-Type Onnuri balance?
**A.** Yes! You can use your linked Onnuri card at official online traditional market malls—including **Korea Post Shopping (`우체국쇼핑 온누리전통시장관`)**, **Onnuri Good Day (`온누리굿데이`)**, and **Onnuri Market**—to order Korean beef (`한우`), fresh fruit, seafood, and regional specialties delivered straight to your door at a 10% instant discount.

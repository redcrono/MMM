## 1. 2026 Chuseok Holiday Schedule & Substitute Holiday Rules

The 2026 Chuseok official holiday schedule unfolds as follows:

* **Chuseok Eve:** Thursday, September 24
* **Chuseok Day:** Friday, September 25
* **Day After Chuseok:** Saturday, September 26
* **Sunday:** September 27

> [!NOTE]
> **Is there a Substitute Holiday (대체공휴일) on Monday?**
> Under South Korea's Regulations on Public Holidays, a substitute holiday for Chuseok is triggered only if one of the three statutory holiday days (Eve, Day, After) overlaps with a **Sunday**. Because the 2026 holiday spans Thursday, Friday, and Saturday, **no substitute holiday occurs on Monday, September 28**. The total break is an uninterrupted **4-day holiday from Sept 24 to 27**.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents (expats) use Onnuri Vouchers and claim the 30% refund?
**Yes, absolutely.** Any foreign resident holding an Alien Registration Card (ARC) or domestic bank account can download the 'Onnuri Pay' app, link their domestic credit/debit card, and buy vouchers with the 15% discount. For the 30% traditional market cash refund, simply present your physical ARC and purchase receipt at the market redemption booth.

### Q2. Do large hypermarkets (E-Mart, Costco, Homeplus) accept Onnuri Vouchers?
**No, they do not.** Onnuri Vouchers are exclusively designated for traditional markets and designated local shopping districts. However, large supermarkets are running parallel government discount coupon promotions (30% to 50% off fresh produce), so comparing prices between traditional markets and hypermarkets is recommended.

### Q3. How do the free highway tolls work during the holiday?
All vehicles entering or exiting expressway tollgates between **00:00 on Thursday, September 24 and 24:00 on Sunday, September 27** are automatically charged **₩0**. Hi-Pass lane users will hear the voice prompt "Toll: 0 Won," and regular tollgate users simply collect and return their transit ticket without payment.

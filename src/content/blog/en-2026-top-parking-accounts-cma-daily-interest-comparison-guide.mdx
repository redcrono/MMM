## 1. Stop Leaving Cash in 0.1% Commercial Bank Checking Accounts

Standard demand deposit checking accounts (보통예금) at South Korea's traditional commercial banks pay just **0.1% p.a.** Leaving ₩30,000,000 of emergency funds, Jeonse moving funds, or stock dry powder in a 0.1% account earns a negligible ₩30,000 per year.

By moving that exact same cash into a **Parking Account (파킹통장)** or **Brokerage Cash Management Account (CMA)** paying **3.4% to 3.8% p.a.** with zero lock-up period and instant 24/7 transfers, your ₩30,000,000 generates **over ₩1,020,000 per year** while remaining 100% liquid at a moment's notice.

> **`Daily Parking Interest (Pre-Tax) = End-of-Day Closing Balance × \frac(Annual Stated Yield)(365)`**

## 3. Decoding the Four Types of Korean Brokerage CMAs

When opening a Cash Management Account (CMA) at a Korean securities firm, you will be asked to choose one of four underlying structures:
1. **RP-Type (Repurchase Agreement - Most Popular):** Your cash automatically buys AAA-rated government and public-enterprise bonds with a guaranteed repurchase rate. Offers instant 24/7 ATM/transfer liquidity.
2. **Issued-Note Type (발행어음형 - Highest Yield):** Exclusive to Korea's four mega-investment banks licensed by the FSC (**Korea Investment & Securities, Mirae Asset, NH Investment, and KB Securities**). Typically pays **0.10%p to 0.20%p more** than standard RP-type CMAs.
3. **MMW-Type (Money Market Wrap):** Managed under a wrap contract and deposited daily with **Korea Securities Finance Corp (한국증권금융 - AAA rated)**. Automatically settles and reinvests both principal and interest every single business day for true automated daily compounding.
4. **Merchant Bank Type (종금형):** Available through Woori Investment & Securities (formerly Woori Comprehensive Finance), this is the **only CMA type covered by the ₩50,000,000 KDIC government deposit insurance**.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents open Internet Bank Parking Accounts or Brokerage CMAs in Korea?
While some mobile-only internet banks restrict non-face-to-face onboarding for foreign Alien Registration Cards (ARCs), foreign residents can easily open high-yield parking accounts and CMAs through **Tier-1 commercial banks (Hana, Shinhan, KB, Woori)** and major **brokerages (Samsung Securities, Mirae Asset, NH Investment)** by visiting a branch once with their ARC, passport, and proof of employment/residence, and then managing RP/Issued-Note parking 100% inside the mobile app.

### Q2. Why do people split Toss Bank / K-Bank Safebox balances into multiple smaller pockets?
Under Korean bank tax withholding rules, fractional Won amounts (< ₩1) on daily interest and the 15.4% withholding tax are truncated (절사), and daily tax amounts under **₩10** (corresponding to roughly **₩64** of daily pre-tax interest) qualify for **Small Tax Exemption (소액부징수)** under the Income Tax Act. However, for most savers, simply clicking "Claim Interest Now" once a day or letting monthly compounding run delivers virtually identical annual returns without administrative hassle.

### Q3. Can I lose principal in a KOFR or CD Rate Parking ETF?
Unlike standard bond ETFs that fluctuate when interest rates rise, overnight rate ETFs like `TIGER KOFR금리액티브` accrue **1/365th of the risk-free overnight repo rate into the share price every single day**. Unless South Korea's benchmark policy rate turns negative (below 0.0%), the ETF's net asset value (NAV) steps up every single day like a staircase. Just remember that selling an ETF requires **T+2 business days** before you can withdraw the cash to a bank account!

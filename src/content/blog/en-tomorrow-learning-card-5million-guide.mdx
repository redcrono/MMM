For university graduates, job seekers, corporate employees pursuing career pivots, and entrepreneurs in South Korea, the Ministry of Employment and Labor (MOEL) provides <strong>up to 3,000,000 to 5,000,000 KRW in government vocational education subsidies</strong> through the <strong>Tomorrow Learning Card (국민내일배움카드)</strong>.

The card subsidizes <strong>45% to 100% of tuition costs</strong> across thousands of state-accredited programs—from full-stack web engineering, artificial intelligence, and cloud DevOps to tax accounting, UX design, and culinary certifications.

This master guide details 2026 eligibility standards, 100% free K-Digital Training (KDT) bootcamps, monthly living stipends of up to 316,000 KRW, and HRD-Net application procedures.

## 2. K-Digital Training (KDT): 100% Free Bootcamps + Cash Stipends

Intensive software bootcamps (AI, Full-Stack Python/React, Cloud Infrastructure) accredited under K-Digital Training are <strong>100% fully state-subsidized (0 KRW tuition)</strong>, even if the total course cost (10M–20M KRW) exceeds your baseline 3M KRW card quota.

### 💰 Monthly Training Allowance (훈련장려금)
Enrollees in full-time courses (&gt; 140 total hours) maintaining ≥ 80% attendance receive:
- <strong>Baseline Living Stipend</strong>: Up to 116,000 KRW/month
- <strong>KDT Special Allowance</strong>: Up to 200,000 KRW/month
- ➔ <strong>Total Monthly Bank Deposit: Up to 316,000 KRW in direct cash!</strong>

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents holding an ARC obtain the Tomorrow Learning Card?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Foreign residents holding permanent residency (F-5 visa), marital visas (F-6 visa), long-term residency (F-2 visa), or overseas Korean status (F-4 visa) are <strong>fully eligible to apply for the card on HRD-Net</strong>.

### Q2. What happens if I drop out of a class prematurely?
Unless caused by legitimate emergencies (employment, verified illness), <strong>unauthorized dropout incurs a 200,000 KRW balance deduction on the 1st offense, 500,000 KRW on the 2nd offense, and card suspension on the 3rd offense</strong>.

### Q3. Can I take classes while receiving Unemployment Benefits (구직급여)?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Completing ≥ 30 hours of Tomorrow Learning Card vocational courses counts <strong>100% as a statutory re-employment job search activity</strong>, fulfilling your monthly unemployment benefit requirements.

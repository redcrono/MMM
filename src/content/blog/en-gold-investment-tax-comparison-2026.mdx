## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can I invest in Gold inside Pension Savings and IRP accounts?
<strong>Yes.</strong> While KRX spot accounts cannot be held in pension wrappers, you can purchase the <strong>`ACE KRX Spot Gold ETF`</strong>, which holds physical spot gold without futures rollover decay, enjoying 100% tax deferral and 3.3%–5.5% pension tax rates.

### Q2. Why is KRX Spot Gold exempt from the 10% VAT on purchase?
Under government market liberalization policies to combat underground gold smuggling, the Korean government created an explicit statutory exemption eliminating the 10% VAT on exchange-traded spot gold until physical vault redemption.

### Q3. US Direct Gold ETFs (GLD, IAU) vs. KRX Spot Gold: Which is better?
Direct US gold ETFs incur a <strong>22% overseas capital gains tax on profits over 2.5M KRW</strong>. For Korean residents, <strong>KRX Spot Gold offers strictly zero tax on unlimited profits</strong>, making KRX spot vastly superior.

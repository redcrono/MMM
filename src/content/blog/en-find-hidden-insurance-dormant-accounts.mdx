## 3. Statutory Statute of Limitations Rules

1. **AccountInfo (Inactive Bank Accounts)**: Inactive accounts with balances ≤ 500,000 KRW can be liquidated and closed in 1 click.
2. **Unclaimed Insurance (3-Year Rule)**: Under the Korean Commercial Code, insurance claims expire **3 years after the occurrence of the benefit event**. However, transferred dormant funds inside the Korea Inclusive Finance Agency can be reclaimed indefinitely.
3. **Card Points (5-Year Expiration)**: Points typically expire on a rolling 5-year schedule. Cashing out annually prevents forfeiture.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents holding an ARC utilize these portals?
**Yes.** As long as you possess a valid Korean bank account, mobile phone number under your Alien Registration Number (ARC), or digital certificate, you can access all 4 recovery portals without restrictions.

### Q2. Does closing inactive bank accounts negatively impact credit scores?
**Zero impact.** Opening or closing standard bank checking/savings passbooks carries zero weight in KCB and NICE credit scoring algorithms.

### Q3. Can I search unclaimed assets of a deceased family member?
**Yes.** The Financial Supervisory Service provides the **Inheritor Financial Transaction Inquiries Service (상속인 금융거래조회서비스)**, cataloging all assets, debts, and insurance contracts of deceased family members in a single comprehensive disclosure.

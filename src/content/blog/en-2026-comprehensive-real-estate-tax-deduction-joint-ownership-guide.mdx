## 1. How South Korea Calculates the November Comprehensive Real Estate Tax

Every year on **June 1 (the statutory assessment date)**, the National Tax Service (NTS) aggregates the **Official Government Published Prices (공시가격)** of all residential properties owned by each individual nationwide. If your cumulative published value exceeds the statutory deduction threshold, you receive a **Comprehensive Real Estate Tax (종합부동산세 - "Jongbuse")** bill in late November, payable between **December 1 and December 15**.

> **`Taxable Base (과세표준) = ( ∑ Published Property Prices - Basic Deduction (₩900M or ₩1.2B) ) × 60%  (Fair Market Value Ratio)`**

Once the NTS applies the progressive tax rate (0.5% to 2.7% for 1–2 home owners; 0.5% to 5.0% for 3+ home owners with taxable bases above ₩1.2B), it **subtracts the overlapping local Property Tax (재산세)** you already paid in July and September so you are never double-taxed on the same value bracket, and finally adds a **20% Rural Special Tax (농어촌특별세)** surcharge on the net payable tax.

## 3. Three Statutory Exceptions That Keep Your 1-Home Status Alive

Even if your household temporarily or involuntarily holds a second residential property on June 1, filing a **Special Exclusion Application (1세대 1주택 특례 / 합산배제 신청)** on HomeTax between **September 16 and September 30** preserves your ₩1.2B deduction and up to 80% tax credit:
1. **Temporary 2-Home Ownership for Moving (일시적 2주택):** If you acquire a new home before selling your old home, you retain 1-Home status as long as you sell the prior home within **3 years** of acquiring the new property.
2. **Inherited Homes (상속주택):** Any inherited residential property is excluded from your home count for **5 years** following the date of inheritance—and if it is a low-value regional home (published price ≤ ₩300M outside the capital/metropolitan area) or a minority share (≤ 40% share), it is excluded **permanently**!
3. **Regional Low-Value Second Home (지방 저가주택):** Owning one additional home outside the Seoul Capital Area and metropolitan cities with an Official Published Price of **₩300,000,000 or less** (or **₩400,000,000 or less** in designated Population Decline "Second Home" regions) does not disqualify your primary residence from 1-Home privileges.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Are foreign nationals owning apartments in Korea subject to the same Comprehensive Real Estate Tax?
**Yes.** Comprehensive Real Estate Tax is a property-based holding tax assessed on the legal owner of record as of June 1 regardless of nationality or visa status. Foreign owners receive the exact same **₩900,000,000 individual basic deduction** (or **₩1.2 Billion** if they are a registered 1-Household 1-Home resident household).

### Q2. Is there any interest or surcharge when I use the 6-Month Installment Payment (분납)?
**Zero interest and zero surcharge!** Unlike income tax extensions, the statutory Comprehensive Real Estate Tax installment program is **100% interest-free**. If your base tax (excluding the 20% Rural Special Tax) is between **₩2.5M and ₩5.0M**, you can defer the amount exceeding ₩2.5M until **June 15 of the following year**; if your base tax exceeds **₩5.0M**, you can defer up to **50%** of the total bill.

### Q3. How does the Senior Payment Deferral (고령자·장기보유자 납부유예) work?
If you are a 1-Household 1-Home owner who is **aged 60+ OR has held the home for 5+ years**, earned **₩70,000,000 or less** in gross salary (comprehensive income ≤ ₩60M) in the prior year, and owes more than **₩1,000,000** in Comprehensive Real Estate Tax, you can pledge a small lien on the property to **postpone paying the tax until you eventually sell, gift, or bequeath the home** (subject to the light statutory interest rate on national tax refunds upon eventual settlement).

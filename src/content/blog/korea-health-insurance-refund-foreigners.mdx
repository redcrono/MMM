Foreigners holding a valid Alien Registration Card (ARC) who are subscribed to the <strong>National Health Insurance Service (NHIS)</strong> in South Korea are entitled to the exact same statutory protections and refund rights as Korean citizens.

One of the most valuable yet underutilized benefits is the <strong>Individual Copayment Ceiling Refund (본인부담상한제 환급금)</strong>, which returns out-of-pocket medical bills exceeding your income bracket back to your bank account in cash.

---

## 1. How the NHIS Copayment Ceiling Works for Foreign Residents

If your total annual covered hospital expenses exceed your designated income ceiling (ranging from approximately 870,000 KRW for lower income to 8,080,000 KRW for top income), <strong>the NHIS automatically refunds 100% of the excess amount.</strong>

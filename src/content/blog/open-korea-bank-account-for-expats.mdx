Opening a bank account in South Korea as a foreign resident or newcomer can sometimes feel overwhelming due to strict Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and voice phishing prevention regulations.

However, once you understand the document requirements and how to lift initial transaction limits, Korean digital banking is among the fastest and most convenient in the world.

## 3. Lifting the "Limited Account" (한도제한계좌) Cap

New bank accounts in Korea are initially opened as **Limited Transaction Accounts** with a transfer limit of 300,000 to 1,000,000 KRW per day.
- **How to lift the limit:** After receiving 3 consecutive monthly salary deposits from your employer or submitting a proof of employment certificate at the bank branch, your account will be upgraded to standard unlimited daily transfers.

To aggressively counter South Korea's demographic headwinds, the government established the <strong>Newborn Special Mortgage & Jeonse Loan Program (신생아 특례대출)</strong> for households who welcomed or adopted a newborn child within the preceding 2 years.

This sovereign policy financing vehicle provides <strong>subsidized interest rates ranging from 1.60% to 3.30% p.a.—nearly half the prevailing commercial mortgage rate—with loans up to 500 Million KRW</strong>.

Crucially, the loan is <strong>completely exempt from Korea's 40% Debt Service Ratio (DSR) ceiling (applying only 60% DTI and up to 80% LTV)</strong>, vastly expanding borrowing capacity for Seoul metropolitan real estate purchases.

This master guide evaluates 2026 expanded income caps (250M KRW), 30-year interest savings calculations, and existing mortgage refinancing guidelines.

## 3. Rate Reductions & Term Extensions for Subsequent Children

- <strong>Rate Discounts</strong>: Additional <strong>0.20%p discount per subsequent newborn</strong> (Statutory floor: 1.20% p.a.).
- <strong>Period Extension</strong>: Special fixed-rate duration extends by <strong>5 additional years per newborn, up to a maximum of 15 years</strong>.

---

## 4. Refinancing (대환) Eligibility for 1-Home Owners

The Newborn Didimdol program explicitly permits <strong>100% refinancing of high-interest commercial bank mortgages for 1-homeowning families</strong>, provided the property is valued under 900M KRW and the birth occurred within 2 years of application.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Are unmarried mothers or cohabitating couples eligible?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Eligibility is adjudicated based on the child's birth certificate and Family Relation Certificate rather than legal marriage status. Single parents are evaluated purely on their individual income.

### Q2. Is there a mandatory physical occupancy requirement?
<strong>Yes.</strong> The borrower must <strong>move in and register their address within 1 month of loan disbursement and maintain physical residence for at least 1 full year</strong>. Failure to do so triggers immediate loan recall.

### Q3. Can foreign nationals married to Korean citizens apply?
<strong>Yes.</strong> If a foreign spouse is registered on the Korean resident registration certificate and has given birth to a Korean citizen child, the household qualifies fully for the 1% mortgage loan.

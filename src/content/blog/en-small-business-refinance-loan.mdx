For self-employed entrepreneurs, independent restaurateurs, and sole proprietors in South Korea burdened by Tier-2 credit facilities (savings banks, capital finance, card loans) carrying <strong>punitive interest rates between 7% and 15%</strong>, the Ministry of SMEs and Startups (MSS) and SEMAS operate the <strong>Small Business Policy Refinancing Loan (소상공인 저금리 대환대출)</strong>.

This sovereign policy vehicle allows qualifying business owners to <strong>refinance high-interest debts up to 100 Million KRW into a 4.50% fixed rate across a 10-year amortization period (including 2 years grace)</strong>.

Refinancing a 50M KRW facility saves over 4 Million KRW annually in pure interest expenses.

This master guide details 2026 eligibility criteria, 10-year repayment schedules, and the digital SEMAS certificate application process.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign entrepreneurs running registered businesses in Korea apply?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Foreign residents holding appropriate business visas (D-8, D-9, F-2, F-4, F-5, F-6) who operate a legally registered sole proprietorship or corporation in Korea are <strong>fully eligible for SEMAS policy financing</strong>.

### Q2. Can personal unsecured loans be refinanced under this program?
SEMAS primarily refinances business-registered loans. However, personal loans can be included if the applicant provides clear accounting documentation proving the borrowed funds were directly deployed for business operations.

### Q3. Are there early repayment penalty fees?
<strong>Zero.</strong> Prepayment penalties are 100% waived by sovereign mandate, allowing business owners to pay down principal whenever liquidity allows.

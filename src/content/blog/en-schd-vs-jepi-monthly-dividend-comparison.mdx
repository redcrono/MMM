## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Does JEPI defend better during market drawdowns than SCHD?
**In mild pullbacks (-5% to -10%), yes.** JEPI's ~8% options premium cash cushion dampens moderate corrections. However, during acute liquidity crises, underlying equity holdings will decline.

### Q2. Does JEPI suffer from principal decay (destructive return of capital)?
**No.** Unlike flawed high-yield closed-end funds, JEPI is backed by J.P. Morgan's high-quality fundamental equity book and has grown its NAV over multi-year cycles.

### Q3. Which Korean-listed SCHD ETF offers month-end payouts?
**`SOL US Dividend Dow Jones (446720)`** distributes dividends on the final trading day of every month, while **`TIGER US Dividend Dow Jones (458730)`** distributes mid-month (~15th).

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can foreign residents open all 3 digital bank accounts?
<strong>Yes.</strong> Registered foreign nationals possessing an Alien Registration Card (ARC) and a Korean mobile phone number under their legal name can open KakaoBank, Toss Bank, and Kbank accounts directly through their mobile apps.

### Q2. How is the 15.4% interest tax deducted with daily interest claims?
Gross daily interest compounds directly into principal. Accumulated tax liabilities are deducted whenever total accrued tax reaches 1,000 KRW or during the monthly scheduled settlement date.

### Q3. How can I safely shelter more than 50 Million KRW?
Divide large cash reserves across institutions: <strong>50M KRW in Kbank, 50M KRW in Toss Bank, and 50M KRW in KakaoBank, capturing 100% KDIC insurance coverage on a combined 150 Million KRW</strong>.

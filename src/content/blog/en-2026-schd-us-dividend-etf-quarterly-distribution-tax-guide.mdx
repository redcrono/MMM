## 2. Tax Comparison: Direct US SCHD vs. Korean-Listed SCHD (ISA / Pension)

Both track the exact same **Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 Index**, but their Korean tax treatment differs fundamentally:

| Tax & Feature Metric | Direct US SCHD (General Account) | K-SCHD (TIGER/SOL/ACE) in ISA | K-SCHD in Pension Savings / IRP |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Distribution Frequency** | **Quarterly (4x/year in USD)** | **Monthly (12x/year in KRW)** | **Monthly (12x/year in KRW)** |
| **Dividend Tax Rate** | 15% US Withholding Tax | 0% up to 2M~4M KRW; 9.9% on excess | Deferred; 3.3%~5.5% Pension Tax at 55+ |
| **Capital Gains Tax** | **22%** on gains above 2.5M KRW/yr | **0% up to cap; 9.9% separate tax** | Deferred; 3.3%~5.5% Pension Tax at 55+ |
| **20M KRW Tax Cliff** | **Included** (Risks 49.5% tax + NHIS loss) | **100% Excluded (Separate Taxation)** | **Excluded** up to 15M KRW/yr payout |

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. If I sell SCHD on the morning of the Ex-Dividend Date, do I still get the dividend?**  
A. **Yes!** Shareholder eligibility is locked in at market close on the day *before* the Ex-Dividend Date. Selling your shares on or after the Ex-Dividend Date does not forfeit that quarter's dividend payout.

**Q2. Why is December's Ex-Dividend Date two weeks earlier than other quarters?**  
A. Charles Schwab advances the Q4 distribution schedule to early December (around Dec 8–11) to complete accounting and tax distributions before Christmas and the calendar year-end close.

**Q3. Which Korean-listed SCHD ETF has the lowest total expense ratio (실부담비용률)?**  
A. TIGER US Dividend Dow Jones, SOL US Dividend Dow Jones, and ACE US Dividend Dow Jones all compete fiercely with headline management fees around **0.01%**, and total all-in expense ratios (including trading and custodian costs) hovering between **0.11% and 0.14% per year**.

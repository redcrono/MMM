With median apartment prices in Seoul comfortably exceeding 1 billion KRW, inheritance tax in South Korea is no longer a concern solely for the ultra-wealthy. Middle-class families owning a single primary residence now regularly receive tax assessments running into tens or hundreds of millions of won.

Because Korean inheritance tax is levied on the deceased person's <strong>entire estate lump-sum</strong> at progressive rates up to 50%, implementing a <strong>disciplined 10-year lifetime gifting cycle can legally eliminate over 100 million KRW in wealth transfer taxes</strong>.

This master guide details the architectural differences between Korean inheritance and gift taxation, statutory 10-year exemption caps, the new marriage/childbirth deduction, and encumbered gifting strategies.

## 2. Korea's 5-Tier Progressive Tax Rate Schedule

The statutory taxable value (Tax Base) is subject to the following brackets:

| Tax Base Bracket (KRW) | Statutory Tax Rate | Progressive Deduction | Effective Calculation Example |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| <strong>Under 100 Million KRW</strong> | <strong>10%</strong> | - | 100M KRW base ➔ 10M KRW calculated tax |
| <strong>100M to 500M KRW</strong> | <strong>20%</strong> | 10,000,000 KRW | 500M KRW base ➔ 90M KRW calculated tax |
| <strong>500M to 1 Billion KRW</strong> | <strong>30%</strong> | 60,000,000 KRW | 1B KRW base ➔ 240M KRW calculated tax |
| <strong>1 Billion to 3 Billion KRW</strong> | <strong>40%</strong> | 160,000,000 KRW | 3B KRW base ➔ 1.04B KRW calculated tax |
| <strong>Over 3 Billion KRW</strong> | <strong>50%</strong> | 460,000,000 KRW | Exactly 50% taxed on every won exceeding 3B KRW |

> <strong>3% Early Filing Deduction:</strong> Filing and paying within statutory deadlines (3 months from the end of the gift month; 6 months for inheritance) secures an immediate <strong>3% deduction on final calculated tax</strong>.

## 3. Statutory 10-Year Gifting Exemption Caps & Marriage Allowances

Under Korean tax law, gifting allowances from the same family group reset <strong>every 10 consecutive calendar years</strong>:

| Relationship to Donor | 10-Year Statutory Exemption | 2026 Special Allowances |
|:---|:---:|:---:|
| <strong>Spouse</strong> | <strong>600 Million KRW</strong> | - |
| <strong>Adult Child / Grandchild</strong> | <strong>50 Million KRW</strong> | <strong>+100M KRW for Marriage/Childbirth (Max 150M)</strong> |
| <strong>Minor Child</strong> | <strong>20 Million KRW</strong> | - |
| <strong>Direct Ascendant (Child ➔ Parent)</strong> | <strong>50 Million KRW</strong> | - |
| <strong>Other Relatives (Son/Daughter-in-law, Nephew)</strong> | <strong>10 Million KRW</strong> | - |

### 💡 100M KRW Marriage & Childbirth Special Gifting Deduction
- <strong>Marriage Window</strong>: Transfers received within 2 years before or after legal marriage registration qualify for an <strong>additional 100M KRW exemption</strong> on top of the 50M base (allowing a newly married couple to receive up to 300M KRW tax-free from both parents).
- <strong>Childbirth Window</strong>: Transfers received within 2 years from childbirth or adoption enjoy the same 100M KRW additional exemption.

## 5. Encumbered Gifting (Burdened Transfer) for Real Estate

When transferring apartments to adult children, an <strong>encumbered gift (부담부증여)</strong>—transferring the property alongside existing tenant security deposits (Jeonse) or mortgages—acts as a powerful tax shield:

- <strong>Mechanics</strong>: The debt portion transferred is treated as a taxable sale by the parent (subject to Capital Gains Tax), while only the net equity is assessed under Gift Tax.
- <strong>Tax Arbitrage</strong>: Splitting one large asset across two separate tax schedules avoids top-bracket marginal rates.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Can we gift assets right before passing away to avoid inheritance tax?
<strong>No.</strong> Under Article 13 of the Inheritance Tax Act, <strong>all gifts made to statutory heirs (children, spouse) within 10 years of death are clawed back and aggregated into the gross inheritance estate</strong>. (5 years for non-heirs like grandchildren or in-laws). Gifting must begin 10 to 15 years prior to be fully effective.

### Q2. Are recurring micro-transfers to my child's account taxable?
<strong>Strictly speaking, yes.</strong> Money deposited into a child's bank account is considered a gift at the time of transfer. Unreported deposits accumulated over years may trigger 20% failure-to-report penalties plus late interest when the child undergoes tax audits during property acquisition. Always formalize transfers via HomeTax.

### Q3. What is the baseline estate tax exemption threshold?
If a surviving spouse is present, the <strong>lump-sum deduction (500M KRW) + minimum spouse deduction (500M KRW) ensures zero inheritance tax on estates up to 1 Billion KRW</strong>. Without a surviving spouse, the threshold drops to 500 Million KRW.

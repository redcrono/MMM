## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Currency-Unhedged vs. Currency-Hedged (H): Which is better for long-term holders?
**Unhedged ETFs (without the 'H' suffix) are vastly superior.** During global economic crises and market crashes, the US Dollar strengthens against the Korean Won, creating a powerful **currency buffer** that cushions your KRW-denominated asset value.

### Q2. Is daily, weekly, or monthly DCA investing superior?
20-year empirical testing demonstrates that the **performance difference between investing once on monthly payday vs. splitting daily purchases is under 0.2% annually**. Automating a recurring monthly transfer on salary day is optimal for psychological consistency.

### Q3. What is the after-tax difference between standard brokerage accounts and Korean ISA/Pension accounts?
In standard accounts, 15.4%–22% dividend and capital gains taxes deplete compounding annually. In an ISA or Pension account, **100% of pre-tax dividends compound unhindered, generating over 100M KRW in additional terminal wealth after 20 years**.

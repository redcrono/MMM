To drastically reduce housing costs for families with newborn children and counter record-low birth rates, South Korea's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT) and the National Housing and Urban Fund (NHUF) operate the **Newborn Special Didimdol Mortgage Loan (신생아 특례 디딤돌 구입자금 대출)**. Following major income limit relaxations, dual-income couples earning up to **₩200 million annually** can now access this ultra-low-interest policy mortgage in 2026.

Any non-homeowning household (or single-homeowner applying for mortgage refinancing) that has given birth to or adopted a child within **2 years prior to the loan application date** can borrow up to **₩500 million** on residential properties valued at **₩900 million or less**, at fixed interest rates starting as low as **1.6% per annum**. Best of all, existing homeowners currently paying 4.5%–5.5% on commercial bank mortgages are 100% eligible to **refinance (대환대출)** into this 1%–2% tier policy loan.

## 1. Newborn Special Home Purchase (Didimdol) vs. Jeonse Deposit (Beotimok) Comparison

Depending on whether your household is purchasing a home outright or signing a two-year Jeonse lease deposit contract, NHUF provides two distinct tracks under the Newborn Special Finance umbrella. Both programs eliminate the marriage certificate barrier—meaning unmarried biological parents and single-parent households with a registered birth certificate qualify equally.

## 2. Interest Rate Tiers, Preferential Discounts & Commercial Refinancing Savings

The base special interest rate for the Newborn Special Didimdol Loan is determined by combined household gross income and loan maturity (10, 15, 20, or 30 years):
- **Combined Income ≤ ₩85 Million:** **1.60% – 2.70% per annum**
- **Combined Income ₩85M – ₩130 Million:** **2.70% – 3.30% per annum**
- **Combined Income ₩130M – ₩200 Million (Relaxed Dual-Income Tier):** **3.30% – 4.30% per annum**

Borrowers can stack **preferential rate discounts (우대금리)** on top of the base rate, driving the final effective interest rate down to the statutory floor of **1.20% per annum**:
1. **Existing Child Bonus:** -0.1%p per child born prior to the 2-year window.
2. **Additional Childbirth After Loan Origination:** **-0.2%p per newborn** plus an automatic **5-year extension** of the special rate period (capped at 3 extensions / 15 years total).
3. **Housing Subscription Savings Account (청약저축) Holder:** -0.3%p to -0.5%p depending on subscription tenure (5–15 years) and payment count.
4. **Electronic Real Estate Contract (부동산 전자계약) & New Apartment Sale:** -0.1%p each.

### Cash-Flow Simulation: Refinancing a ₩400M Commercial Mortgage from 4.5% to 1.8%
Consider a household carrying an existing **₩400 million** commercial bank mortgage at **4.50% per annum** that welcomes a newborn in 2026 and refinances into the Newborn Special Didimdol Loan at an effective rate of **1.80% per annum**:
- **Before Refinancing (4.50% Commercial Mortgage):** Annual interest outlay of **₩18,000,000** (₩1,500,000/month in pure interest).
- **After Refinancing (1.80% Newborn Special Loan):** Annual interest outlay of **₩7,200,000** (₩600,000/month in pure interest).
- **Net Annual Cash Savings:** **₩10,800,000 saved per year (₩900,000/month in immediate disposable cash flow)**, totaling **₩54,000,000 in interest savings** over the initial 5-year special rate window alone!

> **Important Rule on DSR Exemption:** Unlike standard commercial bank mortgages that are strictly restricted by the Stressed DSR (Debt Service Ratio) 40% ceiling, the Newborn Special Didimdol Loan only evaluates **LTV (70%–80%) and DTI (60%)**. Credit loans or existing auto installment debt rarely block approval as long as DTI stays within 60%.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1. Can single-homeowners refinance their existing commercial mortgage at any time after giving birth?**  
A. Yes! Following the regulatory overhaul, the previous restriction requiring the existing mortgage to be at least 1 year old has been abolished. As long as your child was born within 2 years of the application date, you own only **one residential property** valued at ₩900 million or less (based on KB Real Estate market price), and the existing loan was originally taken out as a home purchase mortgage (주택담보대출 용도), you can apply for refinancing immediately. Note that refinancing is capped at the remaining principal balance of your existing mortgage (up to ₩500 million).

**Q2. Are pregnancies (unborn fetuses) or international marriages eligible for the Newborn Special Loan?**  
A. An unborn fetus (태아) alone does **not** qualify at the time of application—the child must be born and officially registered on the Family Relation Certificate (or adoption registration completed) before submitting the application. However, couples who purchased a home using a commercial mortgage during pregnancy can immediately refinance into the Newborn Special Loan right after registering the baby's birth. Multicultural families and expat spouses (such as F-6 marriage migrants or F-5 permanent residents) married to a Korean national are fully eligible when applying jointly.

**Q3. What happens to the interest rate after the initial 5-year special rate period expires?**  
A. If no additional children are born during the initial 5-year period, the interest rate transitions from Year 6 onward depending on your income tier at origination:
- **Combined Income ≤ ₩85 Million:** Rate increases by only **+0.55%p** over your initial special rate (remaining well below commercial market rates).
- **Combined Income > ₩85 Million:** Rate adjusts to the lowest commercial bank monthly average mortgage rate announced by the Bank of Korea at the time of loan origination. Furthermore, there is **zero early repayment fee (중도상환수수료 면제)** after 3 years from loan origination.
